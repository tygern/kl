# Citation audit

Reviewed 9 October 2026 against the manuscript following release v0.5.0. This is an editorial and source audit, not an independent proof verification or a guarantee of priority. The current manuscript has 22 bibliography entries: three were removed and one added. Every remaining entry has an identifiable mathematical or historical purpose.

## Substantive corrections

1. **The supplementary type E degree bound cited a type D lemma.** Gern's Lemma 1.3.8 states parabolic invariance of the a-function in type D. It was an inappropriate reference for this type E application. The argument now cites Lusztig's Proposition 13.8, properties P4 and P12, and their equal-parameter proof in Chapter 15. The degree estimate follows directly from the definition of Delta in §14.1 and P1 proved in §15.2. This also removes the unnecessary chain through Gern's Proposition 1.3.5 to *Cells in affine Weyl groups, II*.
2. **Lusztig's cited text was the 2014 revision, not just the 2003 book.** The bibliography now explicitly links arXiv:math/0208154v2 and identifies it as the source of the locators. The former assertion that P4 and P12 are proved in §15.1 was too narrow: that section introduces the argument; the proof continues through Chapter 15, with the conclusion in §15.6.
3. **Mongelli's leading-coefficient corollary did not itself justify the displayed full polynomial.** The affine D4 example now cites Corollary 3 for the ordinary polynomial, explains why the endpoints are boolean elements, and identifies the specialization as f4. Corollary 2 remains credited for the coefficient 2. The finite D4 example is the f3 specialization. All numbering is explicitly tied to arXiv:1111.2945v1.
4. **The star-preservation reference needed the manuscript's full scope.** Shi's paper concerns Weyl and affine Weyl groups; the manuscript also uses indefinite simply laced groups. Green's *Star reducible Coxeter groups*, Introduction, explicitly states preservation of full commutativity for the star operations in its general Coxeter-group setting. That source now supports the statement, alongside Gern's simply laced formulation.
5. **The affine C historical locator was unreliable.** Laird's Theorem 4.1.5 contains conflicting even/odd wording between statement and proof, and the surrounding discussion also requires care with rank conventions. The manuscript now cites the actual sandwich-stack constructions in §2.3, Figures 2.8–2.9, together with Ernst's §3.2 type-II elements. It does not import the problematic parity assertion or equate all non-cancellable elements with terminals.
6. **Several standard inputs lacked useful locators.** Added references for the subword theorem, faithfulness, the root-sign descent criterion, parabolic factorization, inversion counting, and the affine realization. Parabolic invariance of ordinary KL polynomials now has the precise reference Lusztig §9.6 and Lemma 9.10(c),(d), specialized to z=e. The direct-product formula includes its tensor-product justification. The dihedral polynomial assertion now cites Lusztig Proposition 7.3.
7. **The computation's polynomial convention needed clarification.** The coset computation uses ordinary KL polynomials, with the ordinary recurrence and descent invariance from Björner–Brenti Theorem 5.1.7 and Proposition 5.1.8. It is not an invocation of parabolic KL polynomials.
8. **Historical and computational claims were overstated.** PyCox is a general computational resource; the introduction no longer implies that it only computes cell partitions or character values. The exact failure of FC cell closure and the limitation of the displayed W-graph action are now stated separately. Laird's open-problem list is dated to 2016. The F4/H3/H4 statement describes the scope of the results cited here rather than asserting a verified current open status.

DOIs and available arXiv/handle identifiers are now clickable. Preprint/published numbering differences are explicit. The two Graham attributions remain, but the prose names the sources that report them.

## Verification limits and maintenance

- **Two original sources remain indirectly checked:** Graham's thesis and the 1979 Kazhdan–Lusztig paper. The manuscript now makes the reporting/restatement sources explicit. Before claiming that every original source has been read, obtain those originals and check Graham §9/§9.9 and KL (2.3.e),(2.3.f), Theorem 4.2. This limitation does not leave the operational claims supported only by uninspected texts: Green, Green–Losonczy and Gern supply the inspected statements.
- Public downloads failed for some sources, including Chmutov and Fan, but existing full primary PDFs were available and inspected. A misleading scratch filename `KL79.pdf` proved to contain Casselman's notes, not the 1979 paper, and was excluded as evidence for direct verification. A similarly misnamed “Stembridge98” scratch PDF contained the 1996 paper; the enumeration check used the actual 1998 author text instead.
- Publisher records, title pages and available Crossref responses were used for bibliographic identity. Several Crossref requests were rate-limited; DOI presence is not presented as a guarantee that every link is currently accessible. Online-first dates in metadata are not substituted for the cited journal-volume years.
- Targeted searches did not establish a contrary published result for the manuscript's novelty claims, but absence from search results cannot certify priority or current open status. The novelty sentence remains qualified. No OpenAI combinatorial-invariance result is used by these proofs, so no such citation was reintroduced.
- The new terminal lists, polynomial tables and root families are supported by the manuscript's own arguments and computational certificates. They do not need unrelated external citations. The source audit did not independently re-prove those results or rerun the large computations.
- The native editor successfully compiled the revised source. Citation-key and bibliography checks found no missing, duplicate or unused keys. The source-linked uniform certificate was regenerated after the edits. Release v0.5.1 includes the revised source, matching PDF and this audit; v0.5.0 preserves the preceding snapshot.
