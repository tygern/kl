# Citation audit

The citation review of 9 October 2026 covered the manuscript following release v0.5.0. Its scope was editorial and bibliographic; independent proof verification and historical priority require separate review. At that revision the manuscript had 22 bibliography entries: three were removed and one added. Each retained entry has a mathematical or historical purpose listed below.

## Update for v0.6.0 on 10 October 2026

The v0.6.0 manuscript has 23 bibliography entries, including Biagioli, Costantini and Sasso, [arXiv:2508.08388v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.08388v1), on FC star-irreducibility in affine B and D. That work is cited for context. Its results are not proof inputs and do not include a competing arbitrary-upper-endpoint theorem. Laird's historical locator includes his finite-E and affine-E classification questions (§5.3, p. 63). The fixed-rank FC-finiteness observation cites BJN §5.1. The earlier 22-entry audit below records the preceding citation revision.

For the affine family, five explicit braid witnesses occur in reduced words at every parameter value, by a length-additive translation factorization. BJN's affine-E8 length enumerator and the independently regenerated FC catalogue are supplementary checks, outside the cover argument's dependencies. Historical priority remains outside the audit's verified scope.

## Substantive corrections

1. **The supplementary type E degree bound cited a type D lemma.** Gern's Lemma 1.3.8 states parabolic invariance of the a-function in type D and does not apply to type E. The argument now cites Lusztig's Proposition 13.8, properties P4 and P12, and their equal-parameter proof in Chapter 15. The degree estimate follows directly from the definition of Delta in §14.1 and P1 proved in §15.2. The indirect reference through Gern's Proposition 1.3.5 to *Cells in affine Weyl groups, II* was removed.
2. **Lusztig's cited text was the 2014 revision, not just the 2003 book.** The bibliography now explicitly links arXiv:math/0208154v2 and identifies it as the source of the locators. The former assertion that P4 and P12 are proved in §15.1 was too narrow: that section introduces the argument; the proof continues through Chapter 15, with the conclusion in §15.6.
3. **Mongelli's leading-coefficient corollary did not itself justify the displayed full polynomial.** The affine D4 example now cites Corollary 3 for the ordinary polynomial, explains why the endpoints are boolean elements, and identifies the specialization as f4. Corollary 2 remains credited for the coefficient 2. The finite D4 example is the f3 specialization. All numbering is explicitly tied to arXiv:1111.2945v1.
4. **The star-preservation reference needed the manuscript's full scope.** Shi's paper concerns Weyl and affine Weyl groups; the manuscript also uses indefinite simply laced groups. Green's *Star reducible Coxeter groups*, Introduction, states preservation of full commutativity for the star operations in its general Coxeter-group setting. The references now include that statement and Gern's simply laced formulation.
5. **The affine C historical locator was unreliable.** Laird's Theorem 4.1.5 contains conflicting even/odd wording between statement and proof, and the surrounding discussion also requires care with rank conventions. The manuscript now cites the sandwich-stack constructions in §2.3, Figures 2.8–2.9, together with Ernst's §3.2 type-II elements. The parity assertion is omitted, and non-cancellable elements and terminals are defined separately.
6. **Several standard inputs lacked useful locators.** Added references for the subword theorem, faithfulness, the root-sign descent criterion, parabolic factorization, inversion counting, and the affine realization. Parabolic invariance of ordinary KL polynomials now has the precise reference Lusztig §9.6 and Lemma 9.10(c),(d), specialized to z=e. The direct-product formula includes its tensor-product justification. The dihedral polynomial assertion now cites Lusztig Proposition 7.3.
7. **The computation's polynomial convention needed clarification.** The coset computation uses ordinary KL polynomials, with the ordinary recurrence and descent invariance from Björner–Brenti Theorem 5.1.7 and Proposition 5.1.8. Parabolic KL polynomials are not used.
8. **Historical and computational claims were overstated.** PyCox computes more than cell partitions and character values; its description in the introduction was corrected. The failure of FC cell closure and the limitation of the displayed W-graph action are now stated separately. Laird's open-problem list is dated to 2016. The F4/H3/H4 statement concerns the cited results. Current open status was not verified.

DOIs and available arXiv/handle identifiers are clickable. Differences between preprint and published numbering are listed. The two Graham attributions remain, with the reporting sources named.

## Verification limits and maintenance

- **Two original sources remain indirectly checked:** Graham's thesis and the 1979 Kazhdan–Lusztig paper. The manuscript cites the reporting and restatement sources. Before claiming that every original source has been read, obtain those originals and check Graham §9/§9.9 and KL (2.3.e),(2.3.f), Theorem 4.2. Green, Green–Losonczy and Gern contain the inspected statements used in the arguments.
- Public downloads failed for some sources, including Chmutov and Fan, but existing full primary PDFs were available and inspected. A scratch file named `KL79.pdf` contained Casselman's notes and was excluded as evidence for direct verification of the 1979 paper. A similarly misnamed “Stembridge98” scratch PDF contained the 1996 paper; the enumeration check used the 1998 author text.
- Publisher records, title pages and available Crossref responses were used for bibliographic identity. Several Crossref requests were rate-limited, so current access to every DOI link was not verified. The bibliography retains the journal-volume years, including where metadata lists an earlier online-first date.
- No contrary published result appeared in the targeted novelty searches. Historical priority and current open status require broader verification; the novelty sentence remains qualified. No OpenAI combinatorial-invariance result is used by these proofs, so no such citation was reintroduced.
- The proofs and computational certificates for the new terminal lists, polynomial tables and root families are in the manuscript and supplement. The source audit did not independently re-prove those results or rerun the large computations.
- The native editor successfully compiled the revised source. Citation-key and bibliography checks found no missing, duplicate or unused keys. The source-linked uniform certificate was regenerated after the edits. Release v0.5.1 includes the revised source, matching PDF and this audit; v0.5.0 preserves the preceding snapshot.
