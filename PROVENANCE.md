# Provenance

This file records how the material in this repository was produced. It is a
factual account, written so that readers of the paper, the proof supplement
and the research notes can tell which parts were generated by AI systems,
what the author did, and where the independent review of the work is kept.

## Summary

The project was carried out in October 2026 by Tyson Gern (Initial Capacity,
tyson@initialcapacity.io, ORCID 0009-0003-8288-8786) with substantial
assistance from large language model systems:

- OpenAI's **GPT-6.1 Sol** and **GPT-6 Astra**, run as teams of agents, did the
  literature searches, proposed and drafted the constructions and proofs,
  wrote the enumeration and verification code, produced the internal review
  notes, and drafted the manuscript text. The internal notes refer to the
  subagents as "Astra agents".
- Anthropic's **Claude Fable 5.1** carried out an independent adversarial
  review on 7 October 2026 and, in the revision that followed the review,
  implemented the author's decisions (manuscript restructuring, repository
  hygiene, the v0.2.0 supplement).

The author directed the work, checked the proofs, computations and
references, and takes full responsibility for the content.

## The prompts

The three prompts that drove the first three stages are tracked in `prompts/`.
They are quoted here verbatim.

`prompts/01.txt` (6 October 2026):

> Use the new finding on the Combinatorial Invariance of Kazhdan–Lusztig Polynomials (https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/Combinatorial-Invariance-of-Kazhdan-Lusztig-Polynomials-September-24-2026/paper.pdf) to try to apply the results of my thesis (https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.6074) to other cases/types. Use a team of Astra subagents and some variation of this approach:
> - Find possible applications/results
> - Establish that the result is, in fact, new by deeply researching the field
> - Attempt to prove the new result
> - Verify the proof for correctness and use deep research to make sure everything is cited properly
> - Save any code used for computation along the way (prefer Python/uv)
> - Write up any results in latex

`prompts/02.txt` (6 October 2026):

> Spin off even more agents to find such a family. This would be a big result. Use astra subagents, and make sure each of them take different approaches. Let's do a broad search. This would be huge to find one.

`prompts/03.txt` (6 October 2026):

> Are you able to use the computational approach for E_6 and E_7 to suggest strategies for possible results in E_n?

No other prompts were used for the research stages. The review and revision
stages (below) were run from separate instructions that are summarised here
rather than quoted.

## Stage 1: conditional transfer (6 October 2026, prompt 01)

GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Astra agents read Gern's thesis and the OpenAI
combinatorial-invariance preprint, searched the literature, and produced:

- `research/applications.txt` and `research/literature.txt` (research and
  literature-audit notes);
- `computations/` (exact-integer Kazhdan–Lusztig code for small groups, the
  interval-certificate search `run_search.py`, the $D_6$ recurrence
  certificate and its verifier) and `results/computation.json`,
  `results/d6-recurrence-certificate.json`;
- the research note now archived as `research/archive/notes/kl-transfer.tex`
  (conditional transfer of Gern's type-$D$ results to arbitrary Coxeter
  intervals).

## Stage 2: broad search (6 October 2026, prompt 02)

Nine further Astra agents with distinct assignments
(`research/broad_affine/`, `research/broad_exceptional/`,
`research/broad_surgeries/`, `research/broad_cells/`,
`research/exceptional_referee/`, `research/exceptional_priority/`) searched
for infinite families and for extensions to other types. This stage produced
the finite $E_6/E_7$ terminal classification and its independent matrix
verification (`research/verify_e6_independent.py`,
`research/verify_e7_matrices.cpp`), the affine-$D_4$ example with $\mu=2$
(`research/verify_affine_d4_r.py`), the research note now archived as
`research/archive/notes/star-boundary.tex`, and the status record
`research/broad-search-status.txt`. The agents also found that the star
family is Mongelli's (arXiv:1111.2945, Corollary 2).

## Stage 3: type E (6–7 October 2026, prompt 03)

Three further Astra agents (`research/en_e8/`, `research/en_families/`,
`research/en_literature/`, `research/en_uniform/`, `research/en_affine_referee/`,
`research/en_independent/`) extended the classification to $E_8$ by parabolic
pruning, constructed the rank-uniform $E_{4r+1}$ family, the affine $E_8$
family and the $E_{10}$/$E_{13}$ families, built the FC catalogues, and wrote
the manuscript `results/exceptional-leading.tex`. The status record is
`research/en-search-status.txt`. Two further passes were then run on the
manuscript:

- an editorial pass (`research/editorial/`) that restructured the text as a
  research article; its summary records that it removed the earlier in-text
  mention of the agent workflow, which the present revision restores as a
  "Use of AI tools" section;
- a simulated referee pass (`research/ai-review-notes/`, formerly
  `research/journal-review/`) in which separately prompted Astra agents wrote
  reports on correctness, significance and priority, and the primary agent
  wrote responses and implemented revisions.

The agents then built the proof supplement (`supplement/`), and release
v0.1.0 of the repository was published under the author's GitHub account on
7 October 2026 (12:46 UTC) with the manuscript PDF, the supplement archive
and checksums.

**Machine-generated internal notes are not peer review.** Every text report
and note under `research/` (the review notes in `research/ai-review-notes/`
and `research/editorial/`, the referee-style notes, the search, construction,
proof and literature notes of the `research/*` directories, and the
status/literature files directly under `research/`) carries a two-line banner
saying so; the only `.txt` files without it are the two archival LaTeX source
snapshots `research/ai-review-notes/source-before-followup.txt` and
`research/editorial/source-before-polish.txt`, and data files such as
`checksums.txt` and `research/en_independent/e9-reflection-word.txt`. Headers that originally read "Independent ... referee report" now
read "Separately prompted AI review note". Recommendations such as "minor
revision" in those files are the output of a language model acting on a
prompt, not a journal decision.

## Stage 4: independent review (7 October 2026, Claude Fable 5.1)

Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.1, running an adversarial multi-agent workflow
(71 agents: 14 review dimensions, two verifiers per substantive finding, a
completeness critic and six follow-up investigations), reviewed the whole
repository. Every reviewer wrote its own code and was instructed not to read
the existing AI review notes until the end. The review:

- re-derived every lemma of the manuscript against the primary sources and
  recomputed every computational claim with separately written code,
  confirming the mathematics;
- found that the uniform theorem holds for every $k$ (not only for large $k$),
  that the maximum of $\mu$ over all pairs in $W(E_6)$ is 10 (so the FC
  hypothesis is essential), that the "unresolved" affine $E_8$ odd-gap
  coefficients are computable and lie in $\{0,1\}$, that Table 1 is Gern's
  type-$D$ list plus exactly two new elements, and the structure of the two
  new elements; and recomputed the $D_8$ polynomial;
- identified the attribution corrections (Green's reduction argument, Fan's
  cell theorem, Chmutov's name for Green's question, the known infinite
  $T$-avoiding families) and the missing author, AI disclosure and licenses.

The report is `research/claude-review-2026-10-07/REVIEW.md`; the raw findings
with verifier verdicts are `research/claude-review-2026-10-07/findings-digest.md`;
the reviewers' code and outputs are under
`research/claude-review-2026-10-07/verification-code/`. That directory is
kept verbatim as the review left it: its files contain the reviewers' own
scratch-directory paths, the repository's root `*.log` rule leaves its `.log`
files untracked, and the third-party PyCox copy and the build output inside
its repository snapshot are excluded from version control.

## Stage 5: revision (7 October 2026)

Following the review, the author decided on a shorter, focused paper with a
named author, a "Use of AI tools" section, MIT and CC BY 4.0 licenses, tracked
prompts and this provenance file. The revision was implemented by Claude
Fable 5.1 agents under the author's direction: the manuscript was
restructured, the two research notes were archived under
`research/archive/notes/`, the review's computations were turned into
certificates for release v0.2.0 of the supplement (published 8 October
2026 from commit 39f1f7e), and the repository hygiene items above were carried out. The
addenda dated 7 October 2026 in `research/en_families/report.txt`,
`research/en-search-status.txt`, `research/en_affine_referee/report.txt` and
`research/exceptional_priority/report.txt` record the values the review
computed for questions those notes had left open.

The git history of the repository was squashed into a single commit
(`ab58ab2`, "Publish type E manuscript and reproducible proof supplement") on
7 October 2026 before the v0.1.0 release. SHA-256 values recorded in the
older manifests (`research/ai-review-notes/revision-manifest.json`,
`research/ai-review-notes/followup-manifest.json`,
`research/editorial/revision-manifest.json`,
`research/en_affine_referee/final-source-audit.txt`,
`research/en_uniform/referee.txt` and `referee-certificate.json`,
`results/*-manifest.json`) refer to pre-publication snapshots of the files as

the squashed history, and several of the files have since been edited (for

current files. The two snapshots of the manuscript source kept for this
reason, `research/editorial/source-before-polish.txt` and
`research/ai-review-notes/source-before-followup.txt`, are unmodified.
