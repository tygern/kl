PRIORITY AUDIT: FC-BOTTOM LEADING COEFFICIENTS IN E6/E7 AND STAR GROUPS
Checked 2026-10-07 UTC (2026-10-06 in the user's America/Chicago timezone).
Prepared by the exceptional_priority research subagent. This is a source audit,
not certification that no unlocated prior theorem exists.

DECISIONS

1. The proposed affine-D4/Catalan-star family is NOT a defensible new result.
   Mongelli's 2011 preprint explicitly contains the relevant star-shaped pairs
   and their Catalan leading coefficients. This is substantially stronger prior
   art than merely an old formula for abstract cube intervals. See A below.

2. No checked source states the full proposed finite-E6/E7 assertion with an
   arbitrary upper endpoint and only the lower endpoint fully commutative.
   This remains a candidate extension, not a certified new theorem. Computation
   of E6/E7 cells and cell W-graphs is old. An explicit restricted theorem,
   full coefficient table, and a cell partition are different claims.

3. The present finite-E6/E7 proof should be positioned, at most, as an explicit
   star-reduction proof with reproducible terminal classification. Do not claim
   the first computation of exceptional cells or the first algorithms capable
   of obtaining these coefficients. A publication-level priority claim still
   needs checking full-table computational literature and expert knowledge.

4. Neither the E6/E7 argument described by the parent nor the star calculation
   needs the proposed 2026 full combinatorial-invariance theorem. The star
   family has an explicit 2011 evaluation; cube-lattice KL formulas are older.

A. DECISIVE STAR-FAMILY PRIOR ART (DIRECTLY CHECKED)

Pietro Mongelli, Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials of boolean elements.
arXiv:1111.2945v1, submitted 12 November 2011.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.2945
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.2945
Published J. Algebraic Combin. 39 (2014), 497-525;
https://doi.org/10.1007/s10801-013-0455-8
FPSAC 2013 version: https://dmtcs.episciences.org/2326/pdf

Checked v1 locators: p.4 defines boolean reflections as reduced palindromes
with distinct generators on one side; p.6 defines Catalan-triangle polynomials
and explicitly gives f4(q)=2q^2+3q+1; p.10 Corollary 2 gives nonzero mu via
star diagrams and the Catalan number; p.13 Corollary 3 gives ordinary KL
polynomials. The scope is any Coxeter graph that is a tree.

Exact specialization (our deduction from the printed diagram): let c be the
center and a1,...,am commuting leaves, x=a1...am, w=xcx. Take center labels
(top,bottom)=(1_l,0) and all m leaf labels (2,1_l). The first diagram in
Corollary 2 has h+1=m and gives mu=C_(m/2) when m is even. For m=4 this is
the parent's affine-D4 pair and mu=2. Corollary 3 gives f4. The lower endpoint
is visibly fully commutative. This specialization is immediate prior art,
whether or not Mongelli explicitly calls it a counterexample to Green.

Local evidence: mongelli-2011.pdf, mongelli-2011.txt, mongelli-p10.png,
mongelli-p9.png, mongelli-p11.png in this directory. Diagram verified visually.

B. GREEN'S SCOPE AND THE MEANING OF 'CONJECTURE'

R.M. Green, Leading coefficients of Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials and fully
commutative elements, J. Algebraic Combin. 30 (2009), 165-171.
https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.1650
https://doi.org/10.1007/s10801-008-0156-x
Checked arXiv v1 introduction, printed pp.2-3, and main Theorem 3.1.
Published numbering is Theorem 4.1. The proven result is affine type A with
FC lower endpoint and arbitrary upper endpoint; finite A follows by parabolic
restriction. The introduction speculates about a large class, 'possibly all
of them', of Coxeter groups. It does not restrict this suggestion to finite
or FC-finite groups. It also does not state a numbered universal conjecture.
Calling a star example a counterexample to the universal possibility is fair;
claiming to refute a formally stated all-types theorem is not. The universal
possibility already fails by the elementary specialization in A above.
Local text previously downloaded: /private/tmp/kl-literature/green-leading-2008.txt.

Michael S. Chmutov, The structure of W-graphs arising in Kazhdan-Lusztig theory,
University of Michigan PhD thesis, 2014.
https://deepblue.lib.umich.edu/handle/2027.42/108802
Author full-text upload:
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/295404674_The_Structure_of_W-graphs_Arising_in_Kazhdan-Lusztig_Theory
Checked printed p.33: describes Green's conjecture for a Coxeter system in
terms of outgoing weights from FC vertices. Checked section 2.3, Theorem 2.3.8,
p.58, proof pp.61-62: precisely the FC-lower/arbitrary-upper 0/1 theorem for
type B_n. Equal-parameter C_n is the same Coxeter system. Section 2.3's type-B
hypothesis cannot be dropped from intermediate lemmas, including its use of
Shi. Appendix A quasi-minuscule exceptional W-graphs does not prove the
unrestricted E6/E7 assertion. University direct PDF returned 403; its indexed
text and the author's full-text upload were inspected.

Tyson C. Gern, Leading Coefficients of Kazhdan-Lusztig Polynomials in Type D,
2013 revised thesis. https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.6074
Theorem 4.5.11, printed p.66, proves FC lower/arbitrary upper in D_n.
The exceptional D6 numerical input is already Lemma 4.5.5. The present
E6/E7 proof may reuse it, but should not present that calculation as new.
See the fuller checked numerical locators in ../literature.txt.

C. CLOSE RESULTS WITH DIFFERENT ENDPOINT HYPOTHESES

R.M. Green, Generalized Jones traces and Kazhdan-Lusztig bases,
J. Pure Appl. Algebra 211 (2007), 744-772.
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0509362
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpaa.2007.04.004
Checked arXiv v2 Theorem 7.10 and Remarks 7.12-7.13. The trace formula has
Properties B,F and bipartiteness hypotheses. The simply-laced 0/1 discussion
has BOTH endpoints FC; Remark 7.12 cites Graham for ADE. Remark 7.13 also

Brant C. Jones, Leading coefficients of Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials for Deodhar
elements, J. Algebraic Combin. 29 (2009), 229-260.
https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.1391
https://www.emis.de/journals/JACO/Volume29_2/m102822m2hm2612q.fulltext.pdf
Theorem 1.2 assumes an UPPER Deodhar element, with arbitrary lower endpoint.
Theorem 6.1 explicitly verifies E6,E7,E8,F4,G2 computationally. Deodhar is
stricter than fully commutative. Non-FC terminal tops are not covered.

D. EXCEPTIONAL COMPUTATIONS: WHAT WAS ALREADY AVAILABLE

Meinolf Geck, PyCox: computing with finite Coxeter groups and Iwahori-Hecke
algebras, LMS J. Comput. Math. 15 (2012), 231-256.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.5566
https://doi.org/10.1112/S1461157012001064
Checked Table 2, published p.246 (preprint p.16), and surrounding algorithm.
E6: order 51,840, 652 left cells, 21 graph equivalence classes, about 45 seconds.
E7: order 2,903,040, 6,364 left cells, 56 graph equivalence classes, about 4 hours.
The method constructs cell W-graphs by induction. This establishes old
computability, not a printed theorem bounding every FC-bottom coefficient.
The 'leading coefficients' of character values in section 5 are c_(w,E),
not the KL leading coefficient mu(x,w). Local PDF/text preserved here.

Meinolf Geck and Abbie Halls, On the Kazhdan-Lusztig cells in type E8,
Math. Comp. 84 (2015), 3029-3049.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.6804
https://doi.org/10.1090/MCOM/2963
Checked Remark 2.2, preprint p.4: E6 example prints
'652 left cells (21 non-equivalent), mues:1'; E7 computation takes about four
hours. In context this is a cell-graph computation. It must not be promoted
without further verification to a statement that ALL global E6 mu's are 0/1.
Edges within left cells omit potentially relevant pairs between cells or with
equal descent sets. Remark 4.5 distinguishes abstract irreducible W-graphs.
Introduction p.2 explicitly distinguishes real-group Lusztig-Vogan polynomial
computations from this finite-Weyl-group problem. Local PDF/text preserved.

R.B. Howlett and Y. Yin, W-graphs for the irreducible representations of the
Hecke algebras of type E6 and E7, J. Algebra 321 (2009), 2055-2067.
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021869309000180
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2008.12.018
Checked abstract and scope: constructs irreducible representation W-graphs.
An abstract W-graph realizing a representation is not automatically the
ordinary KL graph on every element. This is not a global-mu bound.

Y. Chen and J.-Y. Shi, Left cells in the Weyl group of type E7,
Comm. Algebra 26 (1998), 3837-3852.
Author full-text upload:
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/243045431_Left_cells_in_the_weyl_group_of_type7
Old indexed author PDF, now returned 404:
https://math.ecnu.edu.cn/~jyshi/myart/e7ychen.pdf
Checked section 2.9: their left-cell graph has left CELLS as vertices, with
adjacencies coming from strings; this is not a full weighted KL graph on W.
Thus 'all left cell graphs' in this source is not the asserted FC-bottom
coefficient theorem. Section 5.13 gives a cell characterization. The scan/OCR
is imperfect; absence of a keyword is not an exhaustive theorem check.
The corresponding earlier E6 cell reference is C. Tong, Comm. Algebra 23
(1995), 5031-5047, as cited by Geck-Halls; not independently read in full.

F. du Cloux, Coxeter software documentation, 'Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials'.
https://www.newliegroups.tech/coxeter/coxeter3/english/kl_e.html
Page last-change date 2010-09-01; local HTML saved. It describes the version-1
strategy of computing full extremal-position tables in finite W and version-3
on-demand calculation. It does not, by itself, document which full E7 table
was actually produced or an FC-bottom theorem. Relevant bibliographic lead:
The state of the art in the computation of Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials,
AAECC 7 (1996), 211-219, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01190330 . Full article was
not inspected; do not claim it ruled out. A software capability is weaker
priority evidence than a published calculation or identifiable data set.

FindStat statistic St001144, largest mu in a Coxeter group.
https://www.findstat.org/StatisticsDatabase/St001144
Checked page data and saved HTML. It records B5/C5 maximum 2, and various
other small classical types. It contains NO E6/E7 entry in the inspected
data. Therefore it neither establishes nor refutes a global E6/E7 0/1 bound.
It is a contributed computational database, not a substitute for the missing
primary E6/E7 coefficient theorem.

E. CELL/REDUCTION LITERATURE AND ADDITIONAL CHECKS

J.-Y. Shi, Fully commutative elements and Kazhdan-Lusztig cells in finite and
affine Coxeter groups (2003), and II, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 133 (2005),
2525-2531, https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-05-07834-2 .
Primary PDFs:
https://math.ecnu.edu.cn/~jyshi/myart/fulcom1.pdf
https://cfc.nankai.edu.cn/_upload/article/files/33/c9/99a45e764e18b406dc60b6f33ed6/8e272b77-5452-43ef-b007-c55b9d861091.pdf
These characterize when the FC set has cell-union/closure properties, using
diagram criteria. Branching types D/E are precisely an important failure
case. Such failures do not imply mu>1, nor do positive closure results imply
the arbitrary-upper bound without additional work.

J.-Y. Shi, Left cells containing a fully commutative element,
J. Combin. Theory A 113 (2006), 556-565.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2005.04.007
Primary mirror:
https://cfc.nankai.edu.cn/_upload/article/files/51/85/f93474b642b19b7452a2005acc83/48ff05ba-d520-4d7a-88dd-5c5aeaed268a.pdf
Main scope is unique minimum elements and connectedness of such cells, not
a theorem stating that all FC-bottom ordinary leading coefficients are 0/1.

R.M. Green and J. Losonczy, Fully commutative Kazhdan-Lusztig cells,
Ann. Inst. Fourier 51 (2001), https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0102003 .
https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/aif.1843.pdf
Example 2.2.5 treats D4 failure of the FC set to be a union of cells. This
must not be mistaken for an affine-D4 mu=2 example or a failure of finite-D
FC-bottom 0/1.

D. Garfinkle and D.A. Vogan Jr., On the structure of Kazhdan-Lusztig cells for

https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-8693(92)90150-K

Checked scanned introduction pp.92-93: D4 Levi/coherent-continuation methods
and operators, main Theorem 2.15 and explicit section-4 calculations. No
FC-bottom theorem found there. Full scan was not exhaustively read; plain
pdftotext is unusable. It remains a background technical source, not a
verified complete priority exclusion. Local scan/image preserved.

Taryn Laird, A Study of T-Avoiding Elements of Coxeter Groups, MS thesis,
Northern Arizona University, May 2016 (advisor Dana Ernst).
https://danaernst.com/scholarship/LairdThesis.pdf
Checked section 5.3, printed p.63 (PDF p.67): explicitly lists classification
of T2-avoiding elements in type E_n as open; also most affine branching types.
T-avoidance concerns absence of noncommuting pairs at either end of reduced
expressions, matching the terminal-element theme of the present approach.
This is positive historical evidence that an E-family classification was
not then part of this project's known results, not evidence that it remained
open in 2026. Searches under T-avoiding, Property T, and exceptional/E6/E7
did not locate a later classification.

F. AFFINE-D4 RELATED OLD WORK, SEPARATE FROM THE DECISIVE MONGELLI RESULT

J.-Y. Shi, Left cells in the affine Weyl group W_a(tilde D4),
Osaka J. Math. 31 (1994), 27-50; received 1 October 1992.
https://www.i-repository.net/contents/osakacu/sugaku/111F0000002-03101-3.pdf
Checked introduction and section 3.4 pp.37-38. Uses the same numbering
(center 2, leaves 0,1,3,4), studies 0134 and its extensions 01342i, 01342ij,
01342ijk. The introduction says its cell classification avoids computing
nontrivial KL polynomials. The inspected text does not assert the specific
mu(0134,013420134)=2. Do not infer such a claim from its cell diagrams.

Pan Chen, Non-trivial Kazhdan-Lusztig coefficients of affine Weyl groups,
arXiv:1804.02353, v1 April 2018, v2 September 2025.
https://arxiv.org/html/1804.02353v2
Checked introduction and Theorem 5.2: affine D_n statement requires n>=5 and
uses mu(xw0,d_w xw0)=n+1, with w0 the finite longest element and dominant
translation parameters. These bottoms have a longest-element suffix and
are not the commuting four-leaf bottom. The type-B/C analogues likewise
concern lowest-cell constructions. They are not needed for the star example.

F. Brenti, A combinatorial formula for Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials,
Invent. Math. 118 (1994), 371-394, Corollaries 6.8 and 6.9.
https://eudml.org/doc/144240
Contemporary extended abstract:
https://fpsac-archive.github.io/FPSAC94/ARTICLES/06Brenti.pdf
Brenti's own later survey p.6 explicitly cites these for Boolean and
cube-face-lattice interval formulas; survey reference 24 identifies the
1994 article. Checked survey primary mirror:
https://www.samuelfhopkins.com/OPAC/files/proceedings/brenti.pdf
Original full article not yet inspected here. Thus the cube polynomial is
old independently of Mongelli; the latter directly settles our actual pair.

G. SEARCH COVERAGE AND LIMITATIONS

Primary sources and authors' or universities' copies were preferred.
Publisher pages, arXiv version histories, and actual theorem scopes were
checked whenever reachable. Search snippets were treated as leads. Searches
included spelling variants E6/E_6/E7/E_7, mu/mues/leading coefficients,
fully commutative, 0-1/zero-one, and the following targeted combinations:

* Kazhdan Lusztig fully commutative E6 E7 leading coefficients
* Geck du Cloux E7 W-graphs; E6 Alvis coefficients maximum
* E6 mu-coefficients; E6 mu PyCox; E7 fully commutative conjecture
* Chmutov E7 fully commutative; Gern exceptional Kazhdan
* Garfinkle Vogan branched Dynkin diagrams 1992
* T-avoiding E6; T-avoiding E_7; T-avoiding exceptional; Property T E7 Coxeter
* fully commutative counterexample Kazhdan; Green conjecture commutative
* affine D4 leading coefficients; exact word 013420134
* Kazhdan Lusztig 1+3q+2q; cube Catalan; cube star
* Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials of boolean elements Mongelli

The cube/boolean-element search found the decisive prior theorem missed by
searching only 'fully commutative'. This illustrates why negative keyword
search results cannot certify novelty. No messages were sent to authors.

Unresolved E6/E7 priority points: whether an older full extremal table already
implies a documented global bound; whether an unpublished/software assertion
explicitly restricts lower endpoints to FC; and whether a post-2016 exceptional
T-avoidance classification exists outside indexed literature. The presently
checked literature does NOT justify asserting these types are an open problem.
