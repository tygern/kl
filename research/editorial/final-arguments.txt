FIX BEFORE FINALIZING

1. Last sentence of the finite theorem proof:
   'All other non-cover coefficients are zero, proving the theorem.'
   This can be read as a new assertion about arbitrary upper endpoints.
   Replace with 'All other non-cover coefficients at terminal upper endpoints
   are zero; Lemma ... therefore proves the theorem.' The asserted 0/1 bound
   is global, but this vanishing description comes from the terminal table.

2. Uniform descent lemma: add the missing sign bridge after the pairing array.
   'Since beta_(r,k) has strictly positive integral coordinates,
   b_(r,k)(alpha_j)=alpha_j-m_j beta_(r,k) is negative exactly when m_j>0.
   Hence R(b_(r,k))=I_r.' For positive m_j, each coordinate is nonpositive
   because m_j and all beta coordinates are positive integers; for m_j<0
   the image is positive. Involutivity then gives L=R. The displayed edge-sum
   test establishes terminality, but not by itself the claimed descent set.

3. The first sentence of the uniform descent proof should explicitly cite
   the lowering witnesses in Appendix Seed data, which establish that gamma
   and gamma+delta are real roots. 'The roots lie in the affine parabolic'
   is too compressed if read as the sole justification of reality.
   Candidate: 'The fixed lowering words in Appendix ... show that gamma and
   gamma+delta are real roots of the embedded affine parabolic.'
   Also define T before it occurs in the lemma statement, or in that statement.

4. The intro uniform theorem no longer says non-fully-commutative, although
   the original theorem did and the revised proof still establishes it.
   Add 'non-fully-commutative' to the theorem sentence to preserve that strength.

5. Explicitly put 'for integers k>=0' in the E10 definition. The subsection
   currently has no stated parameter domain. Likewise say 'Fix an integer
   r>=3' at the start of the uniform construction rather than depending on
   the reader to recover it from the introduction.

6. For the affine k=0 cover test, say 'delete one letter and retain products
   of length 26'. Deleting every letter from a reduced word does not always
   produce a cover; the saved procedure and appendix correctly filter by
   length. This is a small clarification of the algorithm, not a new check.

7. Correct the stray space in 'coefficient assertions .'
