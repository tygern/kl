ACTIONABLE FINDINGS

The companion structural_lemmas.txt supplies two rigorous tools:
(1) A maximum commuting set contained in both descent sets determines the
    FC lower endpoint uniquely, by monomial TL cell intersections.
(2) A reduced finite-parabolic longest factor of length>|I|+1 forces
    mu(i(I),w)=0. This uses ordinary a, and holds for every equal-parameter
    Coxeter group without a boundedness hypothesis. Star equivalence can
    move the upper endpoint before applying the factor criterion.

These tools can replace many numerical lower-endpoint/polynomial checks.
They do not classify the infinite set of arbitrary upper endpoints.

GENERALIZED E_n FOUNDATIONS

Stembridge, On the fully commutative elements of Coxeter groups,
J.Algebraic Combin.5(1996),353--385, published Theorem5.1
(author-preprint Theorem4.1), classifies FC-finite Coxeter systems and
includes E_n for every n>=6. Thus the FC set is finite even for E9/E10.
It says nothing analogous about arbitrary elements or terminal elements.
Author preprint: https://websites.umich.edu/~jrs/papers/FC.pdf
Published primary mirror:
https://www2.math.ethz.ch/EMIS/journals/JACO/Volume5_4/cq9j136368033131.fulltext.pdf
Local: stembridge-fc.pdf and .txt.

Green2006 classifies all generalized E_n as star reducible. This means FC
elements reduce by stars to commuting products. Arbitrary non-FC upper
endpoints may reach other terminals. Green2007 develops their finite
Temperley--Lieb algebra, trace and monomial cells uniformly in n. The
trace's0/1 conclusion retains BOTH-FC hypotheses. Exact proof locators
and deductions are in structural_lemmas.txt.

Hagiwara--Ishikawa--Tagawa, A characterization of the simply-laced FC-finite
Coxeter groups, Ann.Combin.8(2004), uses the same extended family.
An indexed FPSAC2004 primary version, Proposition4.7, gives a loose FC
length bound ell(w)<=2^(n-1)-1. This can cap FC enumeration but cannot cap
terminal upper endpoints. Its KL application concerns the coefficient of
q in P_(e,w) for FC UPPER w, not the top possible coefficient with FC lower.
https://fpsac-archive.github.io/FPSAC04/posters.pdf
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00026-004-0214-6

Green--Xu2020 Proposition3.15 extends ordinary a=heap width on FC elements
from Shi's finite/affine setting to all star-reducible groups. Their later
2023 paper classifies cells of ordinary a-value2 in a(2)-finite groups.
Generalized E_n occur in that framework, but the present E9 terminals have
a>=5, so the a2-cell classification does not solve them. The2023 paper
explicitly separates unconditional P1--P6 from additional boundedness-
dependent statements. This was an important scope check for our factor
lemma: it only uses P1,P4 and a direct parabolic-subalgebra inequality.
2020: https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/alco.95.pdf
2023: https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/alco.275.pdf
Local PDFs/texts: green-xu-a2-classification.*, green-xu-a2.*

UNIFORM0/1: WHAT HAS AND HAS NOT BEEN SHOWN

All generalized E_n have finite FC sets, an explicit diagram algebra,
both-FC leading coefficients in{0,1}, and star-reducibility. None of these
statements alone proves FC-lower/arbitrary-upper0/1. The missing steps
are a classification/control of arbitrary terminals and their remaining
odd-gap coefficients. The maximum-independent-descent lemma plus factor
vanishing gives a concrete route, but there can be terminals with smaller
descent sets or no sufficiently long direct parabolic factor.

The universal all-Coxeter0/1 possibility is already false by Mongelli's
2011/2014 explicit Catalan-star formulas; see the prior report. The affine
D4 four-leaf example does not embed as a standard parabolic in E_n, whose
diagram has only one trivalent vertex. Hence it supplies no immediate
counterexample in this generalized E_n family.
