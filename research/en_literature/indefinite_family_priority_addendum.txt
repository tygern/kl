OUTCOME

No precise predecessor was located for the displayed infinite full-support
terminal reflection families in E10 or E13, or for the stated ordinary
equal-parameter FC-lower coefficient consequence for those families.
Priority is UNCONFIRMED. This is a bounded literature search, not evidence
that no predecessor exists. The mathematical construction is independently
certified by the parent/referee files cited below; its priority requires a
separate assessment. The elementary root formulas, affine translations,
monomial-cell facts, descent obstruction, and parity are established inputs.

The defensible scope is an explicit infinite family of non-FC upper
endpoints, with full support in a fixed indefinite Coxeter group, for which
all FC-lower ordinary mu coefficients lie in {0,1}. It is not a whole-type
theorem for arbitrary upper endpoints of E10 or E13. The constructions use
an affine E9 standard parabolic for the conjugating element; nevertheless
the upper reflections have full ambient support and do not themselves lie
in that proper standard parabolic. Calling the construction wholly
independent of affine methods would be inaccurate.

1. EXACT TARGET UNDER AUDIT

Graph: chain 0--1--...--(n-2), with vertex n-1 attached to 2. Thus the arms
have lengths 1,2,n-4. Normalize simple-root squared lengths to 2.

Embed the affine E9 subdiagram on {0,...,7,n-1}. Extend the vectors
delta=(2,4,6,5,4,3,2,1;3), gamma=(1,2,3,2,2,1,1,0;1)
by zeros in positions 8 through n-2. The semicolon denotes position n-1.
Put T=r_gamma r_(gamma+delta).

E10:
  beta0=(3,7,10,9,7,6,4,3,1,6), I={1,3,5,7,9}, a=1.
E13:
  beta0=(2,3,5,5,4,4,3,3,2,2,1,1,3), I={0,3,5,7,9,11,12}, a=2.
In each case beta_k=beta0-a*k*gamma+(a*k^2+3*k)*delta, b_k=r_(beta_k).

The parent/referee verifies root membership by explicit lowering words,
the exact nilpotent-operator formula for T, strict positivity and distinctness
of beta_k, full support, and terminality with L(b_k)=R(b_k)=I for all k>=0.
Independent sets I have the maximum sizes 5 and 7. Reflection length is
odd; these sizes are odd. The maximum-common-descent lemma below therefore
gives mu(x,b_k)=0 for every FC x with length gap greater than one. Covers
have mu=1. No conjectural formula for ell(b_k) is used in this argument.

2. WHAT IS ALREADY IN THE LITERATURE

R.M. Green, On the Markov trace for Temperley--Lieb algebras of type E_n,
J. Knot Theory Ramifications 18(2) (2009), 237--264.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.0283
  https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218216509006872
  https://experts.colorado.edu/display/pubid_44359
The arXiv preprint is from 2007; the journal publication is 2009. All n>=6
are included, with no finite/affine restriction. Definition4.1, printed
preprint p.9, defines the monomial TL a-function by independent factors.
Proposition4.4(iv),(v), pp.10--11, identifies the right cell of a commuting
product and gives singleton left/right cell intersections. The final
paragraph of Lemma5.2, pp.13--14, already applies essentially the same
intersection argument used here. Theorem1.2/Corollary9.3 concern TWO FC
endpoints. They are not the arbitrary-upper statement under audit.
The exact locators above refer to arXiv v1; journal metadata was separately
checked against the author's institutional record.

The maximum-common-descent argument is a deduction from those old facts:
if x is FC and I is contained in L(x) and R(x), with I maximum independent
in the relevant support, then |I|<=a_TL(x)<=|I|. Thus x shares both
monomial cells with i(I), and x=i(I). The ordinary mu descent obstruction
then leaves only this lower endpoint for a non-cover. If its parity agrees
with that of the upper endpoint, mu vanishes. The deduction does not
identify TL cells with ordinary KL cells. Neither it nor the final parity
step should be promoted as an independently novel foundational theorem.
Detailed proof: structural_lemmas.txt, section1.

J.R. Stembridge, On the fully commutative elements of Coxeter groups,
J. Algebraic Combin.5 (1996),353--385.
  https://websites.umich.edu/~jrs/papers/FC.pdf
  https://emis.de/ft/43562
Published Theorem5.1, p.365, lists generalized E_n for every n>=6 among the
FC-finite groups. The author preprint instead numbers this Theorem4.1,
printed p.15. Do not cite preprint Theorem5.1 for FC-finiteness: that is a
different theorem about parabolic quotients. Stembridge explicitly credits
Fan's thesis for the simply-laced classification. FC-finiteness does not
imply a finite set of terminal arbitrary upper endpoints.

R.M. Green, Star reducible Coxeter groups, Glasgow Math.J.48(3) (2006),
583--609. https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0509363
Theorem5.11 and Theorem6.3 include all generalized E_n. The reducibility
being asserted is for FC elements. It does not classify the non-FC
terminals here. These families are consistent with star reducibility:
a noncommuting terminal cannot be FC in a star-reducible Coxeter group.

3. MONGELLI: EXACT EXCLUSION, AND THE OLDER STAR COUNTEREXAMPLE

P. Mongelli, Kazhdan--Lusztig polynomials of boolean elements,
J. Algebraic Combin.39(2) (2014),497--525; online7June2013;
arXiv preprint2011.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.2945
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10801-013-0455-8
ArXiv v1 p.4 defines a Boolean reflection by a reduced palindrome whose
first half plus centre has distinct generators; a Boolean element lies
below such a reflection. Hence in rank n every Boolean element has length
at most 2n-1. The base lengths101 and75 exceed19 and25. Corollary2, p.10,
already explicitly gives the star-shaped Catalan leading coefficients;
Corollary3, p.13, is the ordinary product formula. Thus the affine-D4
four-leaf mu=2 example is absolute prior art, as documented in the earlier
priority report. Publisher metadata confirms the 2014 volume citation.

Uniform exclusion of EVERY indefinite-family member needs an extra
argument if no all-k length formula is asserted. Base lengths alone do
not establish it. Here is a short self-contained argument:

Every reflection has a reduced palindromic expression p s p^{-1}. If it
has full rank-n support, p together with s uses all n generators, so its
length is at least 2n-1. If the reflection is a Boolean element, the
opposite inequality holds; hence equality holds, and the n letters in
p together with s must be distinct. It is therefore itself a Boolean
reflection. On a simply-laced tree, the root of such a full-support
Boolean reflection has every coefficient equal to1. Indeed, build the
root from the central simple root by the distinct conjugating simple
reflections. Each effective new vertex must attach to the connected
existing support; a tree allows exactly one such neighbour, so the new
coefficient is1. An ineffective step would contradict reducedness of
the palindrome. By induction all coefficients remain1.

Our beta_k[0] equals 2k^2+5k+3 in E10 and 4k^2+4k+2 in E13. It is always
greater than1. Thus no b_k is Boolean. This proof uses no length formula
for the families. A weaker conclusion also follows immediately from
distinctness and the finite rank-n Boolean set: all but finitely many
members are non-Boolean. The stronger root argument is available here.

5. OTHER SCOPE BOUNDARIES AND REMAINING LIMITS

Green0801.1650 proves FC-lower/arbitrary-upper mu0/1 in affine A; Gern
1304.6074 proves finite D. Chmutov's2014 thesis gives finite B/C. None
of these is an indefinite E_n theorem. Jones0711.1391 treats Deodhar
upper endpoints in finite Weyl groups, not these non-FC indefinite uppers.
Precise locators and scope checks are in ../exceptional_priority/report.txt.

Old generalized E_n TL work by Fan/Graham and Green supplies the FC
algebra and cell argument. It cannot automatically be promoted to an
arbitrary-upper KL theorem through the quotient map: the TL quotient
does not simply kill every non-FC KL basis element in these branching
types. See report.txt for the projection-property distinction.

Taryn Laird's2016 thesis, section5.3, printed p.63, listed E_n terminal
classification as an open direction at that time:
  https://danaernst.com/scholarship/LairdThesis.pdf

classification work, not a later E_n classification:
  https://statistics.arizona.edu/sites/default/files/Laird_CV%20Jan%202022_0.pdf
This is historical evidence only, not evidence that the problem remained
open in2026. Terminology such as T-avoiding, T2-avoiding, star-irreducible,
bad elements, and terminal elements is not uniform between authors.
