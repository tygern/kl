NORMALIZED MODULE AND DERIVED CANONICAL RECURRENCE

Use q=v^2, H_s=v^-1 T_s, (H_s-v)(H_s+v^-1)=0, C_s=H_s+v^-1.
Let M=H tensor_(H_J) triv, where H_j acts on triv by v for j in J.
The standard basis is m_u=H_u tensor1 for u in W^J. Multiplication is

 C_s m_u = m_(su)+v^-1 m_u,      if su>u and su in W^J;
 C_s m_u = m_(su)+v m_u,         if su<u;
 C_s m_u = (v+v^-1)m_u,          if su not in W^J.

The last case is called a wall here; it always has su>u. The minimal
representative is then u itself, but this is not ordinary generator
multiplication and must use the displayed scalar.

Let c_v=m_v+sum_(u<v) p_(u,v)m_u be the bar-invariant canonical basis,
where p_(u,v) belongs to v^-1 Z[v^-1] and

 p_(u,v)=v^(ell(u)-ell(v)) S_(u,v)(v^2),
 S_(u,v):=P_(u w_J,v w_J).

For s in L(v), put z=sv. Then z is also right-minimal. Form F=C_s c_z.
Its coefficient at m_v is1. Every coefficient below m_v has no positive
powers of v. Its constant term at m_y is nonzero only if

 y<z, and either sy<y or sy not in W^J,

and equals [v^-1]p_(y,z). Consequently

 c_v=C_s c_z - sum_(y<z; sy<y OR sy not in W^J)
                   ([v^-1]p_(y,z)) c_y.

Proof of the correction rule: the action above can raise a strictly
negative coefficient's exponent only by1. This gives a constant precisely
in the descent/wall branches, from its v^-1 term. Contributions from
m_(su) preserve their negative exponents. The leading m_z term has
sz=v>z in W^J, so creates only m_v+v^-1m_z. Subtracting the displayed
integer multiples of canonical c_y removes all constants, preserves bar
invariance and the leading coefficient, and cannot introduce new constants
at lower elements because the lower coefficients of c_y are strictly
negative. Canonical uniqueness proves the result.

Equivalent q recurrence, with nu(y,z)=[v^-1]p_(y,z):

 S_(u,v)=A_(u,v) - sum_(u<=y<z; sy<y OR sy not in W^J)
                         nu(y,z) q^((ell(v)-ell(y))/2) S_(u,y),

where
 A_(u,v)=S_(su,z)+q S_(u,z),       if su<u;
 A_(u,v)=q S_(su,z)+S_(u,z),       if su>u and su in W^J;
 A_(u,v)=(q+1) S_(u,z),           if su not in W^J.

Set S_(u,v)=0 unless u<=v and S_(v,v)=1. Here nu is the top possible
coefficient of the SPHERICAL polynomial S_(y,z), equivalently ordinary
mu(y w_J,z w_J). Do not substitute ordinary mu(y,z). The correction
exponent is integral whenever nu is nonzero.
