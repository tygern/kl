Exceptional search: complete E6 and E7 lower-FC mu bound
Research date: 2026-10-07 UTC

RESULT

Exact exhaustive enumeration, classical star transport, and Gern's type-D
result give a computer-assisted proof:

  If W has type E6 or E7, x is fully commutative, and w is arbitrary,
  then mu(x,w) belongs to {0,1}.

The proof fragment is e6_e7_proof.inc. Historical priority is unconfirmed.
The proof does not require the September 2026 combinatorial-invariance input.
It also does not construct the originally sought nonclassical D-model interval.
Star operations preserve mu here; no full interval isomorphism or equality of
complete KL polynomials is claimed.

NUMBERING AND TERMINALS

Use chain 0-1-2-3-...-(r-2), with node r-1 attached to node 2.
A terminal (Gern: weakly bad) has no reduced expression beginning or ending
with two noncommuting generators. An eligible bottom is fully commutative,
below the terminal, and has left/right descent sets containing the respective
terminal descent sets. Descent mismatch forces mu=0 outside covers.

E6: 51,840 elements; 72 roots; 662 FC elements; longest length 36.
There are 22 commuting terminals and one other terminal:
  word 1325213, length 7; unique eligible bottom 135, length 3.
The length difference is 4, so mu=0 by parity.

  word 1325436210321432543621324356, length 28;
    bottom 1356, length 4; difference 24; mu=0 by parity.

The length-28 terminal has full E7 support. The length-15 terminal lies in
standard D6 on nodes {1,2,3,4,5,6}. Gern's generators 1,2,3,4,5,6 map to

Thus it is precisely Gern's positive bad pair from Lemma 4.5.5.

CERTIFICATION

The C++ engine uses integral roots and represents each element by its images
of all simple roots, a faithful representation. Breadth-first multiplication
by every generator enumerates the full group and determines length. Root signs
independently check every descent and corresponding length change. The program
also checks every simple involution.

The E7 length histogram matches the product of 1+q+...+q^m for exponents
m=1,5,7,9,11,13,17. All 8,388 right star moves on FC elements preserve FC;
inversion supplies the same number of left moves. Every FC weakly bad element
is a commuting product. Full commutativity is computed using the exact
recursive criterion explained in the proof fragment.

PROOF AUDIT

Induct on the upper length. First dispose of incomparable/equal pairs, even
length differences, and covers. At remaining odd differences d>=3, a descent
mismatch forces mu=0. Otherwise upper descent sets are commuting because they
are contained in the corresponding FC bottom descent sets. If the upper is
not weakly bad, a reduced expression starts or ends with adjacent generators,
giving a decreasing length-3 star. The same star is defined on the FC bottom
and preserves FC. Symmetric mu transport applies. The new difference is d or
d-2, hence stays positive; if the endpoints are incomparable, mu=0, otherwise
the strictly shorter upper permits induction.

All weakly bad terminals have already been handled above. This proof does not
need the old both-FC bound or an unsupported extension of Gern's Proposition
1.1.28 to E: the enumeration checks that FC terminals are commuting products.
Use symmetric mu or explicitly exclude covers when stating star transport;
naive ordered-mu transport can reverse a cover's endpoints.

Definition 2.1.1 and the proof of Theorem 4.5.11 supply the terminal strategy.

Local full text: sources/gern-thesis-2013.txt.

3. Michael S. Chmutov, The Structure of W-graphs Arising in Kazhdan-Lusztig
Theory, Michigan PhD thesis (2014), Section 2.3, Theorem 2.3.8.
https://deepblue.lib.umich.edu/handle/2027.42/108802
Institutional PDF returned 403; the author-uploaded full text was inspected:
