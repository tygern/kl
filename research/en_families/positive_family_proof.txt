AN EXPLICIT POSITIVE INFINITE AFFINE-E8 TERMINAL FAMILY
Research proof, 2026-10-07. Historical novelty is NOT certified.

NUMBERING AND THEOREM

Let W be E9, i.e. affine E8, with simple nodes in the chain
0-1-2-3-4-5-6-7 and an additional node8 attached to2. The affine node is7.
Write alpha_i for the simple roots, with squared lengths2 and adjacent
inner products-1. Put
 delta=(2,4,6,5,4,3,2,1,3),
 gamma=(1,1,1,1,1,0,0,0,0),
 I={1,3,5,7,8}, x=prod_{i in I}s_i,
 b0=(3,1,2,8,7,5,6,4,5,2,3,4,1,2,3,0,1,2,8,5,6,7,4,5,2,3,1).
A tuple of node labels here denotes the corresponding word. Let T be the
root translation a -> a-(a,gamma)delta, and set b_k=T^k b0 T^(-k), k>=0.

THEOREM (using the FC uniqueness lemma specified below).
For every k>=0, b_k is a full-support, non-fully-commutative terminal with
L(b_k)=R(b_k)=I and length27+92k. Its only eligible fully commutative bottom
is x, of length5. Consequently mu(y,b_k)=0 for every FC y. The basic
structural argument proves this for length gaps greater than one; the
finite cover check and FC length bound below eliminate the cover case.
The family is unbounded in one fixed affine Coxeter group.

This is a direct theorem about these explicit upper endpoints. It requires
no 2026 combinatorial invariance. It is not a claim for every affine-E8 upper,
not a classification of affine-E8 terminals, and not a historical priority
claim. The upper endpoints are not fully commutative; k>=1 upper lengths exceed
the maximum FC length44 in the independent complete E9 catalogue.

ROOT CERTIFICATE AND TERMINAL PROOF

The exact root-matrix calculation in positive_affine_family.py establishes:
  ell(b0)=27, b0^2=1, L(b0)=R(b0)=I, b0 terminal;
  delta is in the radical; gamma is a finite-E8 root;
  gamma-b0(gamma)=(0,-2,-3,-3,-2,-2,-1,-1,-2);
  d_j=(alpha_j,gamma-b0(gamma))=(2,-1,1,-1,1,-1,1,-1,-1)_j.
The script stores all nine integer columns of b0 in positive_affine_family.json.
These are finite arithmetic checks on one explicit 27-letter word.

The translation exists in W: T=r_gamma r_(gamma+delta). Both roots are
certified by explicit simple-reflection reduction to a simple root; an
explicit reduced58-letter word for T is included in the JSON. Its action
is exactly a -> a-(a,gamma)delta. Since b0 fixes delta and preserves the form,

  b_k(alpha_j)=b0(alpha_j)+k*d_j*delta.                    (1)

For j in I the base column is negative and d_j=-1; for j outside I the base
column is positive and d_j>0. Thus R(b_k)=I for all k>=0. Conjugation preserves
the involution property, so the left descent set is also I.

For every edge s--t with s in I, the base image b0(alpha_s+alpha_t) is positive,
by the finite terminal check. Moreover d_s+d_t>=0: it equals1 on edge1--0
and0 on all other edges incident to I. Equation(1) therefore implies that
b_k(alpha_s+alpha_t) is positive for every such edge. Since
(b_k s)(alpha_t)=b_k(alpha_s+alpha_t), after deleting a right descent s there
is no adjacent right descent t. The same holds on the left by involution.
This is exactly Gern's weakly bad/terminal condition.

At k=0 the reduced word has full support. At k>=1, each image b_k(alpha_s)
for s in I is strictly negative in EVERY simple-root coordinate, since the
base image is nonpositive and every coordinate of delta is positive. If an
element omitted node i from its support, then its action would leave the
alpha_i coordinate of every vector unchanged (each allowed simple reflection
changes only its own coordinate). The displayed strict negativity contradicts
that for every i. Hence all b_k have full support. Their matrices are distinct
because d is nonzero, and their lengths are consequently unbounded.

ELIGIBLE FC BOTTOM AND COEFFICIENT

Use the following lemma proved in the priority branch from Green0704.0283,
Proposition4.4(iv),(v): if b is supported in E_n, I is a maximum independent
subset of its support, and L(b)=R(b)=I, then its only FC lower endpoint z
satisfying I subset L(z),R(z) is the commuting product x=prod I.
The E9 diagram has maximum independent size5 and I has that size. The parent
also independently enumerated ALL44199 E9 FC elements, with maximum length44;
filtering the two descent masks I yields exactly x. The separate script
catalogue_eligible.py verifies the matrix, FC, and Bruhat predicates on these
filtered candidates. Thus the uniqueness assertion has both a structural
source and a complete finite enumeration cross-check.

The commuting product x is below each b_k: it is the longest element of the
commuting descent parabolic, hence a right factor. Conjugation preserves
length parity, so ell(b_k) is odd and ell(b_k)-ell(x) is even. Therefore
mu(x,b_k)=0. For any other FC y below b_k with length gap>1, at least one
terminal descent is absent from the corresponding descent set of y; the
ordinary descent criterion forces mu(y,b_k)=0. A cover would have mu=1.
Incomparable/equal pairs are treated by the usual conventions.

No FC covers actually occur. At k=0, deleting each letter of the explicit
reduced 27-letter word, then retaining products of length26, enumerates
every Bruhat cover by the strong exchange property. There are21 distinct
covers, none FC. The exact records are in positive_base_cover_check.json;
the parent independently agrees. For k>=1, ell(b_k)>=119, whereas the
complete independent E9 FC catalogue has maximum length44. Therefore
these b_k cannot have FC covers. Combining this finite catalogue bound
and the base cover check with the structural argument gives mu(y,b_k)=0
for EVERY fully commutative y, as asserted in the strengthened theorem.
The 0/1 conclusion needs neither cover computation nor FC length bound.

This argument also shows b_k cannot itself be fully commutative: its two
descent sets contain I, but the unique FC element with those masks is x and
ell(b_k)>5.

OPTIONAL EXACT LENGTH PROOF

The coefficient conclusion above needs only parity, not the length formula.
The formula ell(b_k)=27+92k is additionally certified by affine-root inversion
counting, without enumerating any affine group ball or principal lower ideal.

Take the240 finite roots Phi of the standard E8 parabolic obtained by omitting
node7. For a in Phi let n_min(a)=0 if a is positive, and1 if negative. Every
positive affine real root has a unique form a+n*delta with n>=n_min(a).
Write b0(a)=eta+h*delta with eta in Phi; h is the node7 coordinate of b0(a).
Equation(1) sends a+n*delta to
 eta+(n+h+k*d(a))*delta,
where d(a)=sum_j a_j*d_j. Its image is negative precisely when the affine
level is negative, or the level is zero and eta is negative. Thus the number
of positive roots in this family inverted by b_k is
 max(0,c(a)+k*s(a)),
 c(a)=-h-n_min(a)+[eta negative], s(a)=-d(a).
The stored240-row certificate verifies c(a)>=0 whenever s(a)>0 and c(a)<=0
whenever s(a)<0. Hence every summand is linear or identically zero for all
k>=0. Summing gives constant27 and slope92. This is an exact all-k proof,
not extrapolation from sampled lengths. The script additionally recomputes
reduced words, terminal predicates, and lengths for k=0,...,5 (up to487).

SOURCE AND PRIORITY BOUNDARY

The only non-elementary lower-endpoint input here is the FC uniqueness lemma
from the separate source audit of R.M.Green, On the Markov trace for
Temperley-Lieb algebras of type E_n, arXiv:0704.0283. Ordinary descent criteria
and parity are classical. Historical novelty of this particular infinite
upper family remains for the priority audit; the root-matrix proof does not
establish it. The classical star/Catalan family from research/broad_cells is
a different construction and must not be used as a novelty claim.
