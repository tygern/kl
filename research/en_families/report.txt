PROVED OUTPUT

1. Affine E8 (our E9): an explicit infinite family b_k=T^k b_0 T^(-k)
   of full-support, non-FC terminals, with fixed maximum independent
   left/right descent set {1,3,5,7,8}. Exact length is 27+92k.
   For EVERY FC lower endpoint y, mu(y,b_k)=0.
   The structural proof first gives zero for gaps>1; a finite base-cover
   certificate and the complete E9 FC maximum length44 eliminate covers.
   See positive_family_proof.txt, positive_affine_family.py/.json, and
   positive_base_cover_check.json. Parent independently verified the
   all-k matrix argument, inversion length formula, and cover strengthening.

2. Indefinite E10 and E13: explicit full-support terminal REFLECTION
   families b_k=r_(beta_k), with
     beta_k=beta-a*k*gamma+(a*k^2+3*k)*delta.
   The constants are listed in indefinite_positive_family_proof.txt.
   The descent sets are maximum independent sets of odd sizes5 and7.
   Therefore mu(y,b_k) is0 for every FC y with gap>1, and belongs to{0,1}
   for all FC y. The sole eligible FC bottom is product I; its gap is even.
   The families are distinct and unbounded in their fixed ambient groups.
   All-k proof uses explicit root reductions and the identity
     T(z)=z+(z,delta)gamma-((z,gamma)+(z,delta))delta,
   where T=r_gamma r_(gamma+delta), (beta,gamma)=-3,
   (beta,delta)=-a, a=1 in E10 and2 in E13.
   Exact runner: indefinite_positive_families.py, output .json and .log.
   Parent's independent referee verified both families. Sample lengths
   k=0,...,4 are E10:101,217,333,449,565; E13:75,191,307,423,539.
   No all-k arithmetic length formula is claimed for these two families.

The structural FC uniqueness input is a deduction from R.M. Green,
On the Markov trace for Temperley--Lieb algebras of type E_n,
arXiv:0704.0283, Proposition4.4(iv),(v), and the cell argument at the end
of Lemma5.2. The source explicitly covers all generalized E_n, n>=6.
The priority agent confirms this indefinite scope. It found no exact
predecessor for these explicit families in targeted primary searches;
that does not certify historical novelty. Root translations, descent
sign formulas, the FC uniqueness input, and parity/descent criteria are
standard. Candidate contribution is the explicit full-support terminal
families and resulting FC-lower coefficient theorem.
