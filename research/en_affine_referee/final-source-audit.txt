Proof checks:

* The finite-type star induction excludes covers before transport and
  uses symmetric mu correctly, so starred order reversal causes no gap.
* The E8 pruning argument is exhaustive by unique parabolic decomposition;
  the recursive and D7 implementations provide independent decompositions.
* Green's maximum-independent-set uniqueness deduction uses the monomial
  TL a-function and the singleton left/right cell intersection. It does
  not confuse these cells with ordinary KL cells.
* The affine complete-vanishing theorem properly excludes all cover
  exceptions using the independently certified FC maximum length 44 and
  the 21 base covers, none fully commutative.
* The indefinite all-k argument treats the embedded affine null root as
  non-null in the ambient indefinite form. Its nilpotent operator argument
  justifies quadratic, rather than incorrectly linear, root growth.
* Real-root membership is proved by actual lowering certificates and
  conjugation, not merely inferred from squared norm 2.
* Positivity, fixed descents, terminal root-sum signs, full support, and
  distinctness hold for every k>=0. Reflection parity and the maximum
  independent sets prove the coefficient bounds without an all-k length
  formula.
* Standard-parabolic transfer retains the smaller support; full support
  is only claimed in the native E9, E10, and E13 groups.

The three wording issues identified during this final audit have been
corrected in the source snapshot hashed above:

* Complete-group enumeration is explicitly restricted to E6 and E7.
* The E7-parabolic method is described by its actual minimal-coset
  normalization procedure, rather than incorrectly as the D7 orbit method.
* The Boolean-element comparison uses base lengths only for the base

  members are non-Boolean follows correctly from distinctness and the
  finiteness of the Boolean set in fixed rank.
