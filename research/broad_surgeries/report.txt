Research date: 2026-10-07 UTC. Scope: one explicit family of surgery attempts;

MAIN RESULT
No new family established. There is a useful rigorous negative result for the
most immediate deformation of Gern's rank-eleven interval: changing any one
D6 edge from braid label 3 to 4 and keeping a lifted reduced word of w6 does
not produce any new realization of I6 beyond principal-lower isomorphisms.

EXACT COMPUTATIONAL SCOPE
Use zero-based Gern generators: D6 edges (0,2),(1,2),(2,3),(3,4),(4,5).
Source bottom x6 has commuting word (0,1,3,5), length 4. Source top w6 has
length 15 and word (1,0,2,3,4,5,4,2,1,0,2,3,2,1,0).
Source rank numbers are
  1,12,55,140,248,339,360,287,162,59,12,1
and the interval has 1676 elements.

For each of the five edges, replace its Cartan product 1 by 2. This changes
the Coxeter braid length from 3 to 4. Exhaust all distinct target elements
obtained from ALL source reduced expressions of w6. Exhaustion uses induction
on source right descents, not a sample of expressions. The counts of lifted
tops are 3,3,6,2,2 respectively. All have target length 15.

For every lifted top, exhaust every possible target bottom of length four.
There are 1741 top/bottom cases across these sixteen tops. Every hypothetical
rank-eleven interval with one of those tops must occur in this enumeration.
Rank counts rule out every case except three:
  - One top when (3,4) is inflated, with bottom (0,1,3,5).
  - Both tops when (4,5) is inflated, with the same bottom.
The exact words are in lift-all-bottoms-results.jsonl.

For all three survivors, certify a bijection of entire principal lower ideals,
each with 3184 elements. Generate pairs of elements by the SAME subword mask
in the chosen source/target top words. The relation is one-to-one, onto, and
preserves every length. Thus it is an order isomorphism: if a<=b, choose a
reduced subword for b in the top word and then a reduced subword for a inside
that expression. The paired expressions stay reduced by length preservation;
the subword criterion gives the target relation. Apply the same argument to
the inverse. This certifies full order, not merely equality of rank counts.

The three surviving I6 comparisons therefore extend to [e,w6] isomorphisms.
They are covered by the old result below. They do NOT demonstrate a new use
of the September 2026 arbitrary-interval invariance input.

PRIOR ART BOUNDARY (PRIMARY SOURCES)
Fokko du Cloux, Rigidity of Schubert closures and invariance of Kazhdan-Lusztig
polynomials, Advances in Mathematics 180 (2003), 146-175,
DOI 10.1016/S0001-8708(02)00100-7:
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0001870802001007
Its abstract states preservation of ALL subinterval KL polynomials under
principal-lower isomorphisms when one system has tree/affine-A constituents;
in particular this includes a finite-D source. Consequently a nonprincipal
bottom alone does not escape old invariance if the map extends below it.

E. Delanoy, Combinatorial invariance of Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials on intervals
starting from the identity, J. Algebraic Combin. 24 (2006), 437-463, p.446:
  https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/EMIS/journals/JACO/Volume24_4/g468328324k77711.fulltext.pdf
The reduction to the full case explicitly replaces Coxeter labels by maximal
dihedral lengths visible below the top, cites du Cloux Proposition 3.5, and
preserves all subinterval R-polynomials. This is a direct prior-art warning
against presenting truncated-braid diagram inflation as a new mechanism.

LIMITS / NEXT APPROACH
This screen does not treat tops of different length, bottom words of other
length, simultaneous edge changes, insertions of mandatory letters, or a
source interval other than I6. Changing both endpoint words beyond lifted
source reduced expressions could still produce useful nonprincipal surgery.
No novelty claim follows from this finite failure.

An untested structural thought: folding B_n into D_(n+1), with the B short
generator mapped to the product of the two commuting D fork generators,
changes length by the number of negative entries. A constant-negative-count
B interval could potentially have a D model under this folding. However, full
ambient interval equality and FC of the D bottom would have to be proved;
fixed-point subposet embedding alone is insufficient. The B-wide 0/1 theorem
is already Chmutov's, so only additional exact-polynomial/degree transport or
a genuinely different target family would add useful content. This thought
was not tested and should not be cited as a result.
