Primary source: research/en_literature/green-en-trace.txt, Definition 4.1 and Proposition 4.4(iv),(v), checked with surrounding definitions and hypotheses. Green explicitly treats E_n for all n>=6. The a-function is the maximum size of a reduced commuting factor. In the ambient E_n, any such factor of x has support contained in supp(x), hence in K. Its size is bounded by the independence number of induced K. The descent inclusions supply i(I) as both reduced prefix and reduced suffix, giving equality a(x)=|I|. Proposition 4.4(iv) then gives the right cell, and inversion gives the left cell; Proposition 4.4(v) makes their intersection a singleton containing i(I). The fact that induced K may be disconnected or not itself E-type is NOT a gap: Green is applied in the original ambient E_n, not to K. No equality of TL cells with KL cells, and no equality of the two a-functions in indefinite type, is needed. The manuscript correctly avoids both unsupported identifications.
