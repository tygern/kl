4. WHAT IS SPECIFIC TO THE UNIFORM CONSTRUCTION

The contribution to test against predecessors is this exact combination:
(a) the staircase seed in each rank 4r+1, with its all-r signed-permutation
    real-root certificate;
(b) an affine-parabolic orbit whose roots remain strictly positive and
    whose positive simple pairings give the fixed maximum set I_r;
(c) every edge adjacent to I_r has nonpositive pairing sum, uniformly in
    both r and k, so both terminal conditions survive;
(d) these yield distinct full-support non-FC reflections in every listed
    rank, rather than unchanged proper-parabolic embeddings.

This identifies mathematical content genuinely additional to the cited
general machinery; it does NOT prove first discovery. Real-root membership
is not automatic from norm two in indefinite type. Terminality is not
preserved by arbitrary conjugation. Full support of the uppers is not
lost merely because the translating element lies in an affine parabolic.
Those are the useful features of the proof. Once they and the common
descents are established, the KL consequence is short old-theory reasoning.
The current revised introduction mostly makes this distinction correctly.
