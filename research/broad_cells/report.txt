CELL / TERMINAL SEARCH REPORT
Research date: 2026-10-07 UTC

OUTCOME AND PRIORITY

This search found and proved a sharp affine-D4 obstruction, and an explicit
unbounded Catalan family, but both are covered by older work. They are a
rediscovery and a useful boundary for the current project, NOT a new extension
of Gern's theorem. No new positive infinite family was proved in this branch.

In the simply laced star with center c and m commuting leaves a_1,...,a_m, put
x=a_1...a_m and w=xcx. Then x is fully commutative and

 P_{x,w}(q)=sum_{j=0}^{floor(m/2)} (binom(m,j)-binom(m,j-1)) q^j.

For m=2k, mu(x,w)=Catalan(k). For odd m, mu(x,w)=0 by parity.
The four-leaf case is affine D4 and gives 1+3q+2q^2, hence mu=2.
The cases m>=5 have indefinite geometric form. This does not yield the same
counterexample in every affine D_n: its degree-four graph is not a standard
parabolic subgraph of the higher affine D diagrams.

DECISIVE PRIOR SOURCE

P. Mongelli, Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials of boolean elements,
arXiv:1111.2945v1 (12 November 2011), later J. Algebraic Combin.
39 (2014), 497-525. https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.2945
Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.2945

The upper xcx is a boolean reflection, because the commuting leaves allow
xcx=a_1...a_m c a_m...a_1. Both endpoints are therefore boolean elements in
Mongelli's sense. Corollary 3, printed p.13, covers ordinary polynomials for
all boolean-element pairs in tree Coxeter graphs. Corollary 2, printed p.10,
explicitly supplies Catalan mu values for the star-shaped diagram with leaf
entries (top,bottom)=(2,1_l) and center (1_l,0). The polynomial f_4=1+3q+2q^2
is even displayed on printed p.6. Thus the entire star result, including its
unbounded leading coefficients and affine-D4 instance, is explicitly old.

F. Brenti, A combinatorial formula for Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials,
Invent. Math.118 (1994), 371-394, provides older lattice and cube formulas.
Primary extended abstract (1994):
https://fpsac-archive.github.io/FPSAC94/ARTICLES/06Brenti.pdf
Its Corollary1.15 gives R=(q-1)^rank on lattice intervals. The interval here
is the face lattice of a cube, so classical combinatorial invariance already
suffices. The direct proof below does not even require that general theorem.
Brenti's 2024 survey, printed pp.3,6, cites the full paper's Theorem6.3 and
Corollaries6.8-6.9 for the relevant lattice/Boolean/cube results:
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/382934246_Some_open_problems_on_Coxeter_groups_and_unimodality

Green's 0801.1650 introduction, printed pp.2-3, discussed the possibility of
an all-Coxeter FC-bottom 0/1 statement as computational speculation. It was

ELEMENTARY PROOF OF THE ENTIRE STAR INTERVAL

Let A_L=prod_{i in L}a_i. Every A_L c A_R is reduced of length |L|+|R|+1.
Indeed A_L c sends a leaf root alpha_j to
  alpha_c + sum_{i in L}alpha_i + (-1)^[j in L] alpha_j,
which is positive. Right multiplication by a different leaf leaves that
column unchanged; appending every leaf of R therefore increases length.
The initial A_L and c are also ascents.

More generally A_L c A_R sends alpha_j to (-1)^[j in R] times the above
positive root. Its center coefficient recovers R, and its diagonal leaf
coefficient vanishes exactly when j belongs to L. Thus all 4^m pairs (L,R)
give distinct group elements. Together with the 2^m leaf products, they
constitute the whole principal lower ideal [e,w], by the subword criterion.
The group representation is faithful, but only distinguishing its images
is needed for the uniqueness argument.

The elements of [x,w] other than x are exactly the A_L c A_R satisfying
L union R=[m]. Using the fixed reduced word for each upper pair, the subword
criterion and pair uniqueness give
  A_L c A_R <= A_L' c A_R' iff L subset L' and R subset R'.
A c-free subword can produce x exactly when each leaf is chosen once.
The three coordinate states L-only/R-only/both are the two endpoints/free
coordinate of a cube face. Hence [x,w] is the full face lattice of an m-cube,
including the empty face x. It has 3^m+1 elements; relative rank r+1 has
binom(m,r)2^(m-r) elements for 0<=r<=m.

For a nonempty face z=A_L c A_R, common-right-descent R cancellation removes
A_R from x and z; common-left-descent cancellation next removes
A_[m]\R, a subset of A_L. This reduces R_{x,z} to
R_{e,A_(L intersect R)c}=(q-1)^(|L intersect R|+1).
All intervals between nonempty faces are Boolean; their ordinary KL
polynomials equal 1. Ordinary R/KL reciprocity therefore gives, for F=P_x,w,
  q^(m+1)F(q^-1)-F(q)=(q-1)sum_r binom(m,r)2^(m-r)(q-1)^r
                       =(q-1)(q+1)^m.
The KL degree bound determines F and yields the binomial-difference formula.
No new 2026 combinatorial-invariance input is required.

TERMINAL AND ELIGIBILITY DETAILS

For m>=2, both descent sets of w equal all leaves. Removing leaf i on the
right gives w a_i=A c A_[m]\i, which sends alpha_c to
  (m-2)alpha_c + (m-2)alpha_i + (m-3)sum_{j!=i}alpha_j.
This is positive for m>=3. Thus w is terminal for m>=3, on both sides
because w is an involution. The m=1,2 tops are not terminal.

Any eligible FC bottom must be above x, because its right descent set
contains every commuting leaf. For A_L c A_R, the leaf part of its right
descent set is exactly R; the left version is L. Eligibility forces
L=R=[m], giving w, which is non-FC because a_i c a_i is a braid factor.
Hence x is the unique eligible FC bottom for m>=2. The m=1 top has center
as an additional descent and no eligible FC bottom.

COMPUTATION AND INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION

The discovery engine targeted.py implements exact integral simple-root
matrices, root-sign descents, greedy descent length, lifting-property Bruhat
comparison, finite subword lower ideals, the recursive FC criterion, and
ordinary KL descent recurrence. It does not enumerate the ambient infinite
group or a complete root system.

certify_star.py checks m=1,...,8. It verifies lengths, all distinct normal
forms, lower-ideal and interval cardinalities, exact cube rank counts,
terminal/eligibility claims, polynomial formula, and agreement using every
right descent of the upper. It compares all pairs of normal forms with the
predicted componentwise order for m<=4.

Output: star_family_checks.json and star_family_checks.log.
Full evaluated KL dependencies for affineD4:
  affine_d4_kl_certificate.json (integral matrices, words, lengths, descents,
  polynomial coefficients, all correction terms, root recurrences).

The parent independently used faithful affine signed permutations, direct
subword Bruhat comparison instead of lifting, and R/KL reciprocity instead
of KL descent recurrence. Its files are outside this branch:
  research/verify_affine_d4_r.py
  results/affine-d4-independent.json
It verified all271 nontrivial reciprocity identities for the 272-element
lower ideal. The interval itself has82 elements and ranks(1,16,32,24,8,1).
The parent presentation results/star-boundary.tex was audited here: the
normal-form uniqueness, whole-poset proof, and R cancellation are correct.

BOUNDED ADDITIONAL SEARCH AND NEGATIVE EVIDENCE

colors, layers1,...,21, for generalized E6,...,E16 and affineD4,...,affineD12.
It uses exact matrices and does not enumerate their lower ideals. No
noncommuting terminal was found among these full-color E words. This only
rejects this particular construction; it gives no E classification.

for these five words, not other cell arguments or longer words.
Saved: parabolic_factor_checks.json.
