FINAL SIGNIFICANCE REASSESSMENT OF REVISED MANUSCRIPT
2026-10-07. Reviewed actual results/exceptional-leading.tex after revision.
No main-manuscript edits made by this reviewer.

OVERALL JUDGMENT

The revision now presents a coherent and defensible specialist research
paper. It has addressed the principal positioning objections in my first
review: finite ADE completion is explicit, the finite proof comes first,
the lower-endpoint uniqueness is mathematical rather than another black-
box enumeration, and the infinite families have a clear terminal-obstruction
motivation. The old mechanisms are credited and the endpoint scopes are
accurate. This is a material improvement in the substance visible to a
reader, not merely a stylistic improvement.

My recommendation remains major revision / not yet an acceptance
recommendation, but its grounds are now narrower: publication depends
on establishing the contribution against the literature and furnishing
the promised auditable computational proof. I would no longer ask for
another wholesale rewrite of the narrative. A new difficult KL theorem
is not logically required for a publishable paper if the finite exceptional
completion and classification are previously undocumented.

I have not rerun all certificates and do not certify mathematical
correctness of every computational assertion. This assessment should be
combined with the independent mathematical and reproducibility reviews.

WHAT IS NOW WORKING

1. The finite simply laced corollary makes the real coverage immediately
   apparent. The direct-product degree argument is appropriate and does
   not overreach to all finite Weyl or all crystallographic groups.
2. The terminal table followed by the analytic uniqueness corollary is
   stronger exposition than counting eligible FC endpoints. It identifies
   what the computer proves and what existing cell theory proves.
3. The E8 exhaustion argument states the parabolic-pruning lemma and the
   coset-closure argument. The contribution is recognizably a small terminal
   classification yielding an all-upper theorem, not a vast unreported KL
   polynomial computation.
4. Green's Lemma 5.2 provenance and Laird's dated historical statement are
   appropriately included. The paper does not turn a 2016 open direction
   into an unsupported claim about what is open in 2026.
5. The infinite-family paragraph explains why infinitely many full-support
   terminals matter to the reduction strategy. It plainly describes the
   descent and parity proof, so the reader is not led to expect unknown
   odd-gap non-cover KL computations.
6. The short interval-transfer paragraph accurately labels a formal
   consequence and supplies no inflated claim of new interval models.
   Taking CI as unconditional instead would not change the significance
   assessment: the observation is still formal.
7. The D4 example remains an effective explanation of why leading-mu
   vanishing has limited implications for complete polynomials.

SMALL ACTIONABLE POINT IN THE ACTUAL REVISED TEXT

The abstract currently says, 'We also use Green's monomial cell theory to
construct full-support terminal reflections ...'. The construction is
root-theoretic; Green's theorem proves its coefficient consequence. Change
this to 'We also construct ... . Green's monomial cell theory implies ...'
or equivalent. This preserves the distinction already made correctly in
the introduction. It is a local accuracy correction, not a major defect.

The planned comparison with prior exceptional-type computations and
the precise reproduction-scope paragraph will help. In particular, make
clear that pre-existing cell/W-graph computations are not automatically
the all-pairs FC-bottom theorem, while not implying that no earlier
exceptional computations existed. An advertised reproduction command
must be described in terms of what it actually reruns, separately from
checking saved certificates and separately from symbolic all-rank proof.

REMAINING PUBLICATION LIMITS

The revised contribution statement ('complete certified exceptional
terminal classification ... explicit rank-uniform terminal construction')
is substantially more persuasive than the former blanket disclaimer.
Nonetheless, search failure still does not prove priority. The current
'historical priority remains unconfirmed' sentence is candid. For an
actual submission, the author should be prepared to explain why this
classification/proof is a worthwhile addition even if computations or
observations circulated previously. A referee may know an unindexed
predecessor. This cannot be repaired by stronger wording or by automatically
promoting the infinite-family theorem to the main result.

The finite result remains the publication anchor. Completing a natural
finite ADE statement with a transparent, reproducible exceptional proof
can justify a concise specialist paper. The infinite construction adds
a credible second component, particularly its simultaneous all-rank
root-membership and terminal-sign argument. Its leading-coefficient
conclusion is still easy once the descents are known, and it still does
not address arbitrary affine/indefinite uppers. Those limitations are
now explained fairly in the manuscript.

More parity-zero examples, effective eventual-vanishing thresholds, or
changing CI from conditional to unconditional would not fundamentally
raise the contribution level. If the finite completion is discovered to
be already known with an equally usable proof, the remaining family
construction alone would need a stronger independent significance case;
the present revision does not erase that contingency.

appropriate subject matches, based on their official scopes checked in
the first review:
https://alco.centre-mersenne.org/

The revised manuscript now makes that fit easier to assess. ALCO remains
an ambitious, defensible submission rather than an acceptance prediction.
JAC is also plausible. The substantive threshold is a documented useful
research contribution, not merely correct examples in the journals'
subject area. A stable public code/certificate archive and a careful
novelty discussion remain prerequisites for a confident submission case.

This review therefore supports proceeding with focused final verification
and submission preparation, without claiming acceptance readiness or
inventing a stronger theorem. The revised paper has a plausible standalone
publication case, conditional on its actual novelty and proof audit, and
is no longer undermined by its original result hierarchy.
