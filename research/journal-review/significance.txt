INDEPENDENT JOURNAL SIGNIFICANCE REVIEW
Date: 2026-10-07. Manuscript: results/exceptional-leading.tex.
Scope: substantive contribution, literature positioning, publication threshold.
This is an editorial assessment, not an independent verification of all code.

RECOMMENDATION: MAJOR REVISION BEFORE SUBMISSION

There is a plausible specialist research paper here, primarily because the
finite E6/E7/E8 theorem treats every upper endpoint and could complete the
finite simply laced story. I would not recommend acceptance of the present
version. The paper presently gives too little explanation of its position
in that story, and gives the explicit infinite families disproportionate
weight as KL results. If the finite theorem/classification is already known,
the remaining material would face a substantially higher significance hurdle.
This review cannot certify historical priority. A focused search finding no
predecessor is not proof of novelty, and a blanket disclaimer of priority
does not itself establish a publishable contribution.

The appropriate assessment is neither 'parity makes this trivial' nor
'infinitely many full-support examples guarantee significance.' Constructing
the terminals is substantive; their ordinary leading-coefficient conclusion
is a short consequence of old theory once their descents are known.

RESULT HIERARCHY

1. Main potential contribution: the all-upper finite exceptional theorem,
   together with the complete terminal classification and a reproducible
   E8 search small enough to audit. This is much stronger in coverage than
   an infinite sequence of specified uppers. There is a coherent short
   paper centered on completing finite simply laced types.
2. Secondary structural contribution: an explicit infinite full-support
   family of non-FC terminal reflections at each rank E_(4r+1), r>=3,
   with a symbolic all-rank proof. Full support matters: these are not
   unchanged examples embedded in a growing parabolic. The D signed-
   permutation realization provides a useful transparent root certificate.
3. The maximum-descents lemma and parity criterion are useful organizing
   tools, but immediate deductions from established monomial-cell theory
   and the ordinary KL descent criterion. Attribute them accordingly.
4. Affine E9 supplies a stronger all-coefficients-mu-zero assertion and an
   exact length formula. Keep it as a worked complementary application.
   The E10 family is a small-rank supplement; its proof is repetitive and
   could be shortened or moved to an appendix.
5. Interval transfer is a direct corollary of combinatorial invariance.
   Even if CI is taken as an unconditional established theorem, this
   remains a formal closure observation. Without a new family of cross-
   type interval isomorphisms, it contributes essentially no independent
   publication weight. Make it a brief remark, not a headline result or
   a section that appears to advertise an additional research advance.

PRIMARY-SOURCE COMPARISON AND HONEST SCOPE

Green, Leading coefficients ... and fully commutative elements (2009),
https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.1650 and https://arxiv.org/pdf/0801.1650,
proves the FC-lower/arbitrary-upper assertion in affine A (hence finite A).
Its introduction explicitly raises wider Coxeter scope, and Proposition
2.1 gives the familiar descent/star mechanisms. Gern's 2013 thesis,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.6074, Theorem 4.5.11, gives finite D. The
manuscript is properly an extension of that approach rather than a new
general method for leading KL coefficients. The already checked Chmutov
2014 thesis, Theorem 2.3.8, is finite B/C in the same orientation:
https://deepblue.lib.umich.edu/handle/2027.42/108802.

An immediate useful strengthening of presentation is a corollary for ALL
FINITE SIMPLY LACED WEYL GROUPS. The irreducible assertion combines A,D,E.
For reducible groups, use the ordinary KL product formula: if m components
have positive gap and total gap is d, the product degree is at most
(d-m)/2. When m>=2 this cannot produce the coefficient in degree (d-1)/2;
when m=1 the coefficient reduces to that one component. Full commutativity
is componentwise. This is a genuine clarification of the scope obtainable
without inventing a new theorem. Do not say 'all finite Weyl groups' or
'all crystallographic groups' without separately addressing F4/G2, and
do not say that the paper resolves arbitrary affine or indefinite uppers.

Green's generalized E_n trace paper,
https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.0283 and https://arxiv.org/pdf/0704.0283,
has the precise old cell input: Definition 4.1 and Proposition 4.4.
Moreover the FINAL PARAGRAPH OF LEMMA 5.2 (printed pp.13-14) already uses
the same commuting-prefix/suffix and singleton left/right monomial-cell
intersection argument. Cite that passage as additional provenance for the
maximum-common-descent lemma. Its KL statements assume both endpoints FC;
they do not themselves establish the non-FC-upper theorem in this paper.
Jones's Deodhar-upper theorem likewise has different endpoint hypotheses:
https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.1391, Theorem 1.2 and exceptional Theorem 6.1.

Fresh check of Taryn Laird's 2016 thesis, A Study of T-Avoiding Elements
of Coxeter Groups, §5.3 printed p.63:
https://in.nau.edu/department-mathematics-statistics/wp-content/uploads/sites/377/2018/08/LairdThesis-ek.pdf
lists generalized E_n T2-avoiding classification as an open direction.
The definition of T-avoiding agrees with this manuscript's terminal
condition. The thesis already discusses infinite generalized-F examples.
This is valuable historical context, but cannot establish the status in
2026. Cite it and use established terminology at first occurrence; avoid
implying that the first ever infinite terminal family is obtained here.

The existing local focused audit research/en_uniform/literature.txt
correctly distinguishes FC enumeration, FC star-irreducibility, reflection
enumeration, and arbitrary non-FC terminal classification. It also records
that existing affine translations and D sign changes are old mathematics.
My independent fresh searches did not locate an exact predecessor for the
finite bound or the all-rank staircase family. That finding remains
bounded: non-indexed computations, theses and unpublished observations
are realistic gaps. No claim of certified novelty follows.

WHY THE INFINITE RESULT IS MATHEMATICALLY LIMITED

For each displayed upper b, descent containment eliminates almost every
FC lower x; Green's old cell argument leaves a single commuting product;
parity then gives zero. Thus the paper never confronts an unknown odd-gap
non-cover leading coefficient in its new infinite families. It does not
bound full KL polynomials or explain the general infinite E_n FC-lower
problem. The D4 example P=1+2q is therefore an excellent scope safeguard.

Terminality is not used in the parity criterion, as the manuscript notes.
Accordingly its value must be motivated separately: the family provides
explicit irreducible obstructions to the length-decreasing upper-endpoint
star strategy. It shows that the finite proof's terminal list cannot be
replaced in these infinite groups by a finite list. That is a stronger
explanation of why the construction belongs in this paper than presenting
its zero leading coefficients as if they were difficult KL calculations.

Eventual vanishing at fixed rank uses old FC-finiteness plus unbounded
length. It is useful but should not be sold as an asymptotic KL theorem.
An effective k-threshold or a length formula would improve usability, but
neither alone would change the main significance assessment. More ranks,
more translated seeds, or more parity-zero examples also would not by
themselves supply the missing conceptual advance.

PRACTICAL REVISION THAT USES ONLY ESTABLISHED RESULTS

- Put finite exceptional types immediately after the reductions, before
  the lengthy uniform construction, matching the introduction's hierarchy.
- State the finite simply laced corollary and explain explicitly which
  cases come from prior work. Position the main contribution as the
  certified exceptional classification plus its coefficient consequence.
- Add one clear paragraph explaining what E8 parabolic pruning contributes
  beyond running an existing exhaustive group calculation. The mathematical
  exhaustion argument must be in the paper; independent programs and hashes
  support it, but do not replace it. Archive the actual source/certificates
  under a stable public identifier for submission.
- Introduce terminals as the established T-avoiding/weakly bad elements,
  citing Gern and Laird. Explain that infinite terminal obstructions are
  the reason for the construction. Separate the structural theorem from
  its easy coefficient corollary, or make that relationship explicit.
- Attribute the maximum-descent mechanism directly to Green's existing
  proof. Retain the D4 polynomial example and precise endpoint scopes.
- Shorten E10 and collapse interval transfer to a remark. These changes
  concentrate the paper; they do not magically make a known result new.
- Replace a bare global priority disclaimer with a precise contribution
  statement and careful citations. It is acceptable to say 'we give a
  complete certified classification ... and derive ...'; it is not yet
  justified to say 'first' or 'settles a longstanding open problem'.

JOURNAL FIT AND PUBLICATION THRESHOLD

Algebraic Combinatorics is a subject fit: its official scope includes
active interplay between algebra and combinatorics, explicitly including
root systems and representation theory:
https://alco.centre-mersenne.org/ . Journal of Algebraic Combinatorics has
similarly relevant official aims:
https://link.springer.com/journal/10801/aims-and-scope . Its publication of
Green's 2009 result is relevant precedent for subject and scale, but not
an entitlement to publish a later exceptional completion.

My editorial judgment is that ALCO would be an ambitious but defensible
target only after the contribution/priority case and reproducible finite
proof are made convincing. JAC is also a plausible specialist target,
with the same unresolved novelty requirement. These are scope-based
assessments, not acceptance predictions or claims about current editors'
preferences. Neither journal's stated scope guarantees that the present
parity families clear its significance threshold.

ALCO's current author instructions permit a standard LaTeX PDF for initial
submission and recommend amsart and BibTeX; no need to spend revision effort
on the final journal class now:
https://alco.centre-mersenne.org/page/instructions-for-authors/ .

A concise paper proving a previously undocumented finite ADE completion
with auditable exceptional classification, plus a well-motivated uniform
terminal construction, could be publishable without a stronger KL theorem.
If that finite completion turns out to be known, a standalone paper on
the present examples would probably need more: a classification, a general
construction theorem, a meaningful non-parity coefficient computation, or
an application requiring these particular families. Those are potential
future projects, not results to imply in this revision. Merely promoting
the infinite families or CI corollary in the abstract would not suffice.
