EXHAUSTIVE E8 TERMINALS BY RECURSIVE PARABOLIC PRUNING

Outcome

The search is complete, not length-bounded. It finds exactly 2,160 elements
of W(E8) with no reduced expression ending in two noncommuting generators.
Exactly 64 also have no reduced expression beginning in such a pair. These
two-sided terminals consist of 58 products of commuting generators and six
non-fully-commutative elements. The six lengths are 7,8,8,15,28,50. Each has
one eligible fully commutative lower endpoint, with respective length gaps
4,4,4,11,24,46.

The recursive algorithm tests 143,660 candidate products across all ranks,
and never enumerates or stores the full E8 group or full E7 group. The
recorded E8 run used approximately 6 MB peak resident memory. Runtime and
resource measurements are recorded in e8-recursive.json; they are machine-
specific, not complexity guarantees.

Files

Diagram and terminology

Use E8 generators 0,1,2,3,4,5,6 in a chain, with 7 adjacent to 2.
For a subset K of the simple generators, write W_K for its standard
parabolic subgroup. An element is right terminal relative to K if it has
no reduced expression in W_K ending in two noncommuting generators.
Two-sided terminal means the same condition for w and w inverse.

The exact right-terminal test, for each s in K with w(alpha_s) negative,
requires w(alpha_s + alpha_t) positive for every adjacent t in K. This is
equivalent to the reduced-expression definition: s is a right descent of w,
and t is a right descent of ws, precisely when w admits a reduced suffix ts.
Since s(alpha_t)=alpha_t+alpha_s for adjacent simply laced generators, the
test is a sign test on root sums. The implementation checks the left
condition by applying this same test to w inverse.

Completeness lemma

Let J subset K. Every w in W_K has a unique standard parabolic decomposition
w=a*v with a in W_K^J (the minimal right-coset representatives), v in W_J,
and length(w)=length(a)+length(v).

If w is right terminal relative to K, then v is right terminal relative to
J. Indeed, a forbidden reduced suffix ts in v remains a forbidden reduced
suffix in the length-additive concatenation a*v. Equivalently, a preserves
the signs of all roots in the J root subsystem, because a is a minimal
right-coset representative. Thus the right-terminal test for s,t in J
has the same result in w and v.

Consequently the following procedure gives ALL right-terminal elements of
W_K: take every a in W_K^J and every right-terminal v in W_J, form a*v,
and apply the right-terminal test for K. No element can be missed, and
unique parabolic decomposition guarantees that no product is duplicated.
The recursive program additionally asserts uniqueness of accepted states.

Minimal coset enumeration

Starting from the identity representative, multiply on the left by each
generator in K. Repeatedly remove right descents in J to obtain the minimal
element of that right coset. Each removal strictly lowers Coxeter length,
so it terminates; absence of right descents in J characterizes the unique
minimal representative. Store each resulting representative once and
continue breadth-first closure. Since the generators in K act transitively
on the right cosets, this visits every coset. The process never truncates
by length or by a candidate cap. Expected coset cardinalities are asserted
as a separate check.

The program computes full root permutations of each representative and
explicitly verifies positivity on all positive roots in the J subsystem.
It also checks the resulting reduced lengths and inverse words of every
retained right-terminal element.

Fully commutative bottoms

The code independently generates all fully commutative elements by ascent
BFS. A candidate w is FC iff its right descent set commutes and every
descending predecessor ws is FC. This is an exact simply laced criterion:
a forbidden braid lies either in a proper reduced prefix or at the suffix;
in the latter case its braid relation produces adjacent right descents.
All shorter FC levels have already been generated when the criterion is
applied. Prefixes of FC words are FC, so restriction to FC ascents loses no
FC element. The E8 count is 10,846.

Every left and right length-three star move on an FC element is checked to
remain FC. The counts are 3,620 in E6, 16,776 in E7, and 77,476 in E8.
For every non-FC terminal b the program tests every FC x for both descent
inclusions and x <= b using the exact lifting recurrence for Bruhat order.
No upper or lower length cutoff is used.

Implication for the leading-coefficient theorem

Parity makes the five even-gap terminal coefficients zero. The sole odd-gap
case is supported on {1,2,3,4,5,7}, a standard D6 parabolic. Gern's generators
(1,2,3,4,5,6) map to E8 labels (1,7,2,3,4,5), exactly as in E7 with branch
label changed. It is the same (x6,w6) pair, with mu=1. Combined with the
previously audited terminal-reduction argument and standard-parabolic KL
invariance, this proves the ordinary equal-parameter FC-lower-endpoint
0/1 assertion for E8 as well. This is a computer-assisted mathematical
conclusion. No historical priority claim is made.

Independent D7 implementation audit

I read research/en_independent/e8_d7_cosets.cpp. Its parabolic J={1,...,7}
is D7, with 322,560 elements and longest length 42. Its vector
beta=(4,7,10,8,6,4,2,5) has simple-coroot pairings delta_{s,0}, so it is
the fundamental weight at node 0. Its stabilizer in W(E8) is exactly W_J.
Thus its 2,160-point orbit parametrizes all right cosets, independently of
the minimal-representative normalization used above. Latest-first path
recovery correctly realizes the left-reflection BFS, and the explicit
absence of right descents in J verifies minimality.

The composition formulas, inverse order, root-sum terminal test, FC
recurrence, and Bruhat comparison are correct. I found no blocking error.
This implementation tests 280 D7 right-terminal factors times 2,160 cosets,
or 604,800 products. It independently obtains exactly the same 2,160 right
terminals, 58 commuting terminals, six non-FC terminals, and eligible
bottoms. The matrix cross-check verifies equality of the actual group
elements for all six rows. It also checks the complete D7 length histogram
and the convolution of the D7 and coset histograms against the E8 Poincare
polynomial, coefficient by coefficient.

Reproducibility status

Both methods validate against the existing independent complete E6 and E7
enumerations. The E8 flat and recursive methods terminate cleanly with all
assertions enabled. A preliminary /usr/bin/time -l wrapper encountered a
sandbox restriction on sysctl after the program itself had completed; a
direct rerun exited zero, and all saved certificates come from clean direct
runs. The recursive binary records peak RSS through getrusage directly.
