1. EXACT TARGET AND ITS LIMITS

The graph is the chain 0--1--...--(4r-1), with branch 4r attached to 2.
For r>=3 put n=4r+1, a=2r-4, and
  beta_0=r-1, beta_1=r, beta_2=2r-1,
  beta_j=floor((4r+1-j)/2) for 3<=j<=4r-1, beta_(4r)=r.
Embed affine E9 on {0,...,7,4r} and use
  delta=(2,4,6,5,4,3,2,1;3), gamma=(1,2,3,2,2,1,1,0;1),
with zero coordinates 8,...,4r-1; the semicolon denotes the branch.
The roots and reflections under audit are
  beta_(r,k)=beta_r-a*k*gamma+(a*k^2+r*k)*delta,
  w_(r,k)=r_(beta_(r,k)), k>=0.
Their common descent set is the maximum independent set
  I_r={0,3,5,...,4r-1,4r}, |I_r|=2r+1.

The companion construction.txt proves real-root membership through the finite
D_(4r) parabolic, the translation identity, full support, terminality, and
distinctness for every r,k. Its proof is symbolic; finite arithmetic checks
are supplementary. The FC-lower consequence is mu(x,w_(r,k))=0 for every
FC x with length gap greater than one, and hence mu in {0,1} for every FC x.
At each fixed rank all these coefficients eventually vanish, since FC elements
form a finite set and the upper lengths tend to infinity.

This is not a theorem about all arbitrary upper endpoints of E_n, does not
cover every congruence class of ranks, and does not compute full polynomials.
The upper reflections have full ambient support, although the conjugating
translation lies in a proper affine parabolic. They are not merely a fixed
endpoint pair embedded unchanged into larger ranks.

2. EXACT OLD FOUNDATION FOR THE FC-LOWER REDUCTION

R.M. Green, On the Markov trace for Temperley--Lieb algebras of type E_n,
J. Knot Theory Ramifications 18(2) (2009), 237--264.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.0283
  https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218216509006872
The preprint is from 2007; publication is 2009. It includes all n>=6,
including indefinite E_n. Locators in arXiv v1:
  Definition 4.1, printed p.9: a_TL is the maximum independent-factor size.
  Proposition 4.4(iv), pp.10--11: the monomial right cell of i(I).
  Proposition 4.4(v), p.11: a left/right cell intersection in a two-sided
    cell has exactly one element.
  Last paragraph of Lemma 5.2, pp.13--14: essentially the same commuting-
    factor/singleton-intersection argument already appears explicitly.
  Theorem 1.2 and Corollary 9.3 concern TWO FC endpoints. They do not by
    themselves treat the non-FC upper reflections in the present family.

Thus the maximum-common-descent lemma is an immediate deduction from an
old source, not an independently established priority claim. If x is FC,
supp(x) is in J, I is maximum independent in J, and I is in L(x) and R(x),
then a_TL(x)=|I|. Both cell statements identify x with i(I). No identification
between these monomial TL cells and ordinary KL cells is needed.

The ordinary descent obstruction is stated in Tyson C. Gern, Leading
coefficients of Kazhdan--Lusztig polynomials in type D, arXiv:1304.6074,
Proposition 1.2.4, especially part (3): away from a cover, an upper descent
missing from the corresponding lower descent set forces mu=0.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.6074
Reflection length is odd, so an odd maximum common descent set leaves an
eligible bottom of the same parity. This supplies the coefficient conclusion.

3. A SLIGHTLY BROADER DEDUCTION: DIFFERENT MAXIMUM DESCENT SETS

This is a deduction from the same Green proposition, not a novelty claim.
Let b be any upper element of W(E_n), let J=supp(b), and let I,K be maximum
independent subsets of J with I subset L(b), K subset R(b). Put alpha=|I|=|K|.
Among FC lower elements, at most one can contain I in its left descents and
K in its right descents. In fact that unique element in the ambient group is
  x_*=i(I)i(K\I),   ell(x_*)=2alpha-|I intersect K|.

Proof. Each generator occurs once in the displayed word, so it is reduced
and FC. Every member of I intersect K commutes with every member of I union K;
therefore I subset L(x_*) and K subset R(x_*). Its a_TL value is alpha, using
the prefix and the maximum independent-set bound. Proposition 4.4(iv) puts
it in the right cell of i(I) and left cell of i(K). Any other eligible FC x
has a_TL(x)=alpha and lies in that same intersection, so Proposition 4.4(v)
identifies it with x_*. The existence of x_* itself ensures these two cells
belong to the same two-sided cell, as the proposition requires.

Consequently, if ell(b) is congruent to |I intersect K| modulo 2, every
FC-lower noncover mu(x,b) vanishes. If x_* is not below b, they vanish
regardless of parity. Extra upper descents may also make x_* ineligible.
Terminality and involutivity are not needed for this general deduction;
the present reflection family is one way to enforce its hypotheses.

4. WHY THIS DOES NOT DETERMINE THE FULL KL POLYNOMIAL

P. Mongelli, Kazhdan--Lusztig polynomials of boolean elements,
J. Algebraic Combin.39 (2014), 497--525 (online 7 June 2013).
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.2945
  https://doi.org/10.1007/s10801-013-0455-8
Exact locators in arXiv v1: section 3, printed p.6, defines f_h by the
Catalan triangle; Corollary 3, printed p.13, is the ordinary-polynomial
product formula. Corollary 2, printed p.10, gives the corresponding star
leading coefficients and already covers the four-leaf mu=2 example.

For the D4 three-leaf star with center c and commuting leaves a,b,d, put
x=abd and w=xcx. Then ell(x)=3, ell(w)=7, L(w)=R(w)={a,b,d}, and the
common descent set is maximum independent. Mongelli's formula gives
  P_(x,w)(q)=f_3(q)=1+2q,
although mu(x,w)=0 by the even gap. Thus neither P=1 nor a 0/1 assertion
for all polynomial coefficients follows from this parity mechanism. The
example embeds parabolically into every generalized E_n (with proper support).
Its polynomial is an old exact calculation, not a new counterexample.

Mongelli's boolean upper elements in fixed rank n have length at most 2n-1.
His formula therefore cannot cover an infinite sequence of distinct uppers
at fixed rank. This alone excludes all but finitely many members of each
present fixed-rank family from that scope; no indefinite length formula is
required. The old star examples prevent claiming that FC-lower 0/1 holds
for arbitrary Coxeter groups.

5. ROOT-THEORETIC CONSTRUCTION: OLD INPUT VERSUS SPECIFIC FAMILY

Deleting node 0 leaves type D_(4r). The even signed-permutation realization
is standard and is explicitly given in Gern, Example 1.1.6, printed pp.2--3,
which cites Björner--Brenti, Combinatorics of Coxeter Groups, Proposition 8.2.3.
The construction uses the half-integral D weight
  lambda=(-e_1+e_2+...+e_(4r))/2
and the action negating coordinates 2,4,...,4r. Its 2r sign changes are even,
so it belongs to W(D_(4r)). This proves that the seed is in the simple-root
orbit. The staircase coefficients and the terminal inequalities are the
specific data under audit; the spin-weight orbit mechanism is not new.
Norm 2 alone would not certify a real root in an indefinite root system.

Likewise T=r_gamma r_(gamma+delta) and
  T(z)=z+(z,delta)gamma-((z,gamma)+(z,delta))delta
are standard reflection algebra. The quadratic iterate follows because
T(delta)=delta and T(gamma)=gamma-2delta. The embedded delta is not radical
in the ambient indefinite group. These formulas should not be advertised
as a new translation method. The specific seed, signs, and simultaneous
all-r/all-k terminal inequalities are what need comparison to prior work.

Arbitrary conjugation does not preserve ordinary KL polynomials or length.
Conjugation by the longest element of an entire finite Coxeter group
permutes its simple system and does preserve KL data. A longest element
of a proper parabolic generally does not permute the ambient simple system.
In this construction the D action is used to certify real-root membership;
the KL claim is obtained separately from descent sets and parity.

The independent-set number of E_n is ceil(n/2), so its odd parity occurs
exactly for n congruent to 1 or 2 modulo 4. Passing from n to n+4 preserves
this parity but does not by itself preserve terminality or produce maximum
descent sets. A rank-extension assertion requires a root/word construction
and sign proof, supplied here for n=4r+1 rather than inferred from padding.

6. FC FINITENESS, LENGTH BOUNDS, AND EARLIER TERMINAL INVESTIGATIONS

J.R. Stembridge, On the fully commutative elements of Coxeter groups,
J. Algebraic Combin.5 (1996), 353--385.
  https://websites.umich.edu/~jrs/papers/FC.pdf
Published Theorem 5.1, p.365 (author-preprint Theorem 4.1, p.15), includes
all generalized E_n among the FC-finite groups. This suffices for eventual
zero at each fixed rank. It does not make the upper group finite.

M. Hagiwara, M. Ishikawa, H. Tagawa, A Characterization of the Simply-Laced
FC-Finite Coxeter Groups, Ann. Comb.8 (2004), 177--196.
  https://doi.org/10.1007/s00026-004-0214-6
Primary FPSAC 2004 short paper:
  https://media.pims.math.ca/pdf/science/2004/fpsac/papers/Hagiwara.pdf
Proposition 4.7, short-paper p.7 / proceedings printed p.113, states
  max ell(FC(E_n)) <= 2^(n-1)-1.
The exponent was visually verified in the PDF. This is an upper bound,
not an exact maximum formula; journal proposition numbering was not verified.
Thus ell(b)>2^(n-1) suffices to exclude FC covers. No effective k threshold
is obtained merely by citing this bound.

J.R. Stembridge, The Enumeration of Fully Commutative Elements of Coxeter
Groups, J. Algebraic Combin.7 (1998), 291--320.
  https://dept.math.lsa.umich.edu/~jrs/papers/countFC.pdf
  https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1008623323374
The author preprint's sections 3.6 and 4.7 handle the E-series through
formal languages. This is rank-uniform FC enumeration and FC-involution
enumeration, not a classification of arbitrary terminal upper elements.
No sharper exact maximum-length formula was located in that paper during
this audit. This does not establish that no such formula is known elsewhere.

Dana Ernst's author research statement, section 3.1, printed pp.3--4,
  https://danaernst.com/archive/Statements/ResearchErnst.pdf
reports work during 2011--2012 with Ryan Cross, Katie Hills-Kimball, and
Christie Quaranta on an infinite class of noncommuting T-avoiding elements
in generalized type F. Thus an unqualified claim of the first infinite
family of noncommuting terminal elements would be unsafe. The passage does
not state this E_(4r+1) family or an FC-lower ordinary-mu theorem.
The earlier literature audit also checks Laird's 2016 thesis discussion of
E_n T-avoiding classification as a historical open direction. Historical
open-problem statements do not certify its status in 2026.
