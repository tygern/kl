RANK-UNIFORM FULL-SUPPORT TERMINAL REFLECTIONS IN E_(4r+1)
All-parameter proof, 2026-10-07. Historical priority is UNCONFIRMED.

THEOREM AND EXPLICIT DATA

Let r>=3 and k>=0 be integers. Put N=4r and n=N+1. The generalized E_n
diagram is the chain 0--1--...--(N-1), with branch vertex N attached
to2. Every simple root alpha_i has norm2, with inner product-1 on edges.
Write b=N for the branch label when giving reflection words below.

Define the positive integral vector beta_r by its coordinates:
  (beta_r)_0=r-1,  (beta_r)_1=r,  (beta_r)_2=2r-1,
  (beta_r)_j=floor((4r+1-j)/2),  3<=j<=4r-1,
  (beta_r)_(4r)=r.
Thus its long arm is the staircase
  2r-1, 2r-2,2r-2, 2r-3,2r-3, ..., 1,1.
Here the first entry is the coefficient at node3.

Set a=2r-4. In the same coordinate order, put
  delta=(2,4,6,5,4,3,2,1,0,...,0,3),
  gamma=(1,2,3,2,2,1,1,0,0,...,0,1),
where the last coordinate is at branch4r and all coordinates8,...,4r-1
in these two vectors are zero. Finally define

  beta_(r,k)=beta_r-a*k*gamma+(a*k^2+r*k)*delta,
  w_(r,k)=r_(beta_(r,k)),
  I_r={0,3,5,...,4r-1,4r},    x_r=product_(s in I_r) s.

For EVERY integer r>=3 and k>=0:
  (i) beta_(r,k) is a positive real root with all coordinates nonzero;
 (ii) w_(r,k) is a full-support, non-FC terminal element, with
      L(w_(r,k))=R(w_(r,k))=I_r;
(iii) for every fully commutative y in W(E_(4r+1)),
      mu(y,w_(r,k)) belongs to {0,1}, and it is zero if the length gap
      exceeds1. The only eligible FC bottom is x_r; mu(x_r,w_(r,k))=0;
 (iv) for each fixed r the w_(r,k) are distinct and have unbounded length;
  (v) for each fixed r, all these FC-lower coefficients are zero for
      every sufficiently large k.

An eligible bottom means a fully commutative element containing both
upper descent sets in the corresponding lower descent sets. No bounded-r
enumeration, KL-polynomial computation, or FC catalogue is a hypothesis.
This provides full-support examples in unbounded ranks n=13,17,21,...,
not an embedding of a fixed-rank endpoint pair. It claims neither the
bound for arbitrary upper endpoints nor a formula for the whole P_y,w.

1. UNIFORM REAL-ROOT PROOF FOR THE SEED

Delete node0. The remaining standard parabolic is finite D_N, N=4r.
In a Euclidean space with orthonormal basis e1,...,eN realize its roots by
  alpha1=e1-e2,
  alpha_j=e_j-e_(j+1),  2<=j<=N-1,
  alpha_N=-e1-e2.
The branch label N is distinguished from the Euclidean index N here.
Let
  lambda=(-e1+e2+...+eN)/2.
Adjoin a vector h orthogonal to this Euclidean space with (h,h)=2-r,
and realize alpha0=h+lambda. Since (lambda,lambda)=r, the Gram matrix
of these N+1 vectors is exactly the E_(N+1) Cartan matrix: alpha0 has
norm2, pairs-1 with alpha1, and pairs0 with every other simple root.
This realizes the full ambient root space, not just the D_N parabolic.

The coefficient of alpha0 in beta_r is r-1, and direct Cartan pairing
gives (beta_r,alpha0)=r-2. Hence z=s0(beta_r) has alpha0 coefficient1
and every other coefficient unchanged. Substituting the staircase into
the displayed Euclidean realization yields

  z=h+(-e1-e2+e3-e4+e5-e6+...+e_(N-1)-eN)/2.             (1)

For complete coordinate verification: the first Euclidean coordinate is
-1/2+r-r=-1/2, the second is1/2-r+(2r-1)-r=-1/2, and every subsequent
coordinate j equals1/2-(beta_r)_(j-1)+(beta_r)_j, with the convention
(beta_r)_N=0 for this last *long-arm* difference. The staircase difference
is0 for odd j and-1 for even j, giving (1), including j=N.

Let F negate exactly the even Euclidean coordinates2,4,...,N and fix the
others. It negates2r coordinates, an even number, so F belongs to the
even signed-permutation group W(D_N), and fixes h. Explicitly it is the
product, for j=1,...,r, of the double sign changes

  r_(e_(4j-2)-e_(4j)) r_(e_(4j-2)+e_(4j)).               (2)

Both vectors in each factor are D_N roots, and the two orthogonal root
reflections negate precisely those two coordinates. Equation (1) says
z=F(alpha0). Therefore

  beta_r=s0 F(alpha0).                                  (3)

This is a uniform Weyl-group word construction of the seed root for every
r>=3. In particular it proves reality, and hence norm2, without inferring
real-root membership merely from a norm calculation or from finite samples.
One can also write its reflection as w_(r,0)=s0 F s0 F s0, since F^2=1.

2. UNIFORM AFFINE-PARABOLIC TRANSLATION

The nodes0,...,7,N form a standard affine-E8 parabolic. Its null root
is delta. The vector gamma is a root of the finite-E8 subdiagram obtained
by omitting node7; gamma+delta is an affine real root. Their root status
can alternatively be checked by the following fixed reduction words,
which work for every branch label b=N:
  gamma: 2,1,0,4,3,2,1,6,5,4,3,2;
  gamma+delta: the preceding word followed by
    b,2,1,0,3,2,1,4,3,2,5,4,3,6,5,4,7,6,5,b,2,1,0,3,2,1,4,3,2.
Applied in the listed order, each reduces its positive root to alpha_b.

Put T=r_gamma r_(gamma+delta). The identities gamma^2=2, delta^2=0,
(gamma,delta)=0 give the exact ambient action

  T(z)=z+(z,delta)gamma-((z,gamma)+(z,delta))delta.         (4)

This formula is valid even though delta is not radical in E_(4r+1).
Direct substitution of the seed coordinates gives
  (beta_r,gamma)=-r,  (beta_r,delta)=-(2r-4)=-a.
Also T(delta)=delta and T(gamma)=gamma-2delta. Induction in (4) gives

  T^k(beta_r)=beta_r-a*k*gamma+(a*k^2+r*k)*delta.          (5)

Thus every beta_(r,k) is real and w_(r,k)=T^k w_(r,0) T^(-k).
For positivity and distinctness, rewrite (5) as
  beta_(r,k)=beta_r+k*v+binom(k,2)*u,
  v=(3r-4)delta-(2r-4)gamma,  u=2(2r-4)delta.
Both v and u are coordinatewise nonnegative for r>=3, and v is nonzero.
For example the coordinates of v at0,...,7 and the branch are
  (4r-4,8r-8,12r-12,11r-12,8r-8,7r-8,4r-4,3r-4;7r-8),
with zeros elsewhere. Since beta_r is strictly positive, so is every
beta_(r,k). Their heights strictly increase with k.

3. EXPLICIT DESCENTS AND TERMINALITY FOR ALL r,k

Let m_j=(alpha_j,beta_(r,k)). Cartan multiplication gives
  m0=r-2,   m1=2-r,   m2=-1-a*k,
  mj=(-1)^(j+1)*(1+a*k),  3<=j<=7,
  m8=-1-a*k^2-r*k,
  mj=(-1)^(j+1),          9<=j<=4r-1,
  m_(4r)=1+a*k.
Thus m_j>0 exactly for j in I_r. Every edge incident to I_r has the sum
of its two m-values nonpositive: most are0, while the exceptional sums
are1-r-a*k on edge1--2, -a*k*(k-1)-r*k on edge7--8, and
-a*k^2-r*k on edge8--9. All are nonpositive for integer r>=3,k>=0.
The edge1--2 does not meet I_r but is listed to make the entire local
sign pattern explicit.

The reflection formula is
  w_(r,k)(alpha_j)=alpha_j-m_j*beta_(r,k).
Because beta_(r,k) is a strictly positive integral root, this image is
negative exactly for m_j>0. Hence the right descent set is I_r, and the
left descent set agrees since w_(r,k) is an involution. If s in I_r and
t is adjacent, then
  w_(r,k)(alpha_s+alpha_t)=alpha_s+alpha_t-(m_s+m_t)beta_(r,k)>0.
Therefore, after deleting any right descent s, no adjacent t is a right
descent. The same holds on the left. This is exactly the terminal condition
that no reduced word begins or ends with two noncommuting generators.

4. FULL SUPPORT, MAXIMUM INDEPENDENCE, AND THE FC BOTTOM

An element of a standard parabolic omitting node i fixes the alpha_i
coordinate of every vector. The reflection formula above changes that
coordinate for some alpha_j, because the i-th root coordinate is positive
and some m_j is nonzero. Thus every w_(r,k) has full support.

The independent set I_r has size2r+1 and is maximum. Indeed the diagram
contains the matching
  (0,1),(2,4r),(3,4),(5,6),...,(4r-3,4r-2)
of2r disjoint edges, leaving one vertex unmatched. An independent set has
at most one vertex from each matching edge and the unmatched vertex,
giving the bound2r+1 attained by I_r.

Green's monomial Temperley--Lieb cell theorem implies the following
maximum-independent lemma in every generalized E_n: if an FC element y
has I contained in both L(y) and R(y), and I is maximum independent in
the ambient support, then y=product I. For clarity, its proof uses the
initial and final commuting factors to make the monomial a-value exactly
|I|. Proposition4.4(iv) puts y in the corresponding monomial right and
left cells of product I; Proposition4.4(v) makes the intersection a
singleton. No ordinary-cell identification is used. The source explicitly
covers every n>=6, including these indefinite diagrams.

Consequently the only eligible FC bottom of w_(r,k) is x_r. It is below
w_(r,k) because a commuting descent parabolic has its longest element
as a right factor. Its length2r+1 is odd, as is the length of every
reflection. The even length gap implies mu(x_r,w_(r,k))=0. For any other
FC y with gap>1, a left or right upper descent is missing downstairs,
so the ordinary KL descent criterion forces mu(y,w_(r,k))=0. Covers
have mu=1, proving the asserted 0/1 bound for every FC lower endpoint.

The upper elements are not FC: if they were, the same uniqueness lemma
would identify them with x_r, whereas x_r has a(-1)-eigenspace of
dimension2r+1 and each upper reflection has such an eigenspace of dimension1.

5. UNBOUNDEDNESS AND EVENTUAL ZERO

The positive norm2 roots beta_(r,k) have strictly increasing heights, so
they are distinct. Equal reflections in norm2 roots would imply roots
differing by sign, which is impossible here. Thus for each fixed r the
reflections are distinct. Every ball in the finitely generated Coxeter
group is finite, so their lengths tend to infinity as k tends to infinity
(no monotonic length formula is asserted or needed).

Generalized E_n is FC-finite for every n, by Stembridge's classification.
For fixed r let M_r be the maximum FC length. Once ell(w_(r,k))>M_r+1,
no FC Bruhat cover of the upper exists. All noncover FC coefficients
already vanish by the structural proof. Therefore every FC-lower mu
coefficient is zero for all sufficiently large k, for each fixed r.
This statement gives no effective or rank-uniform threshold for k.
